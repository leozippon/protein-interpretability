# Direction-1 capability map: independently gated mechanism assays

**Plan of record, 2026-09-24.** The revised Direction-1 objective is a mechanism-oriented capability map: which biological constraints these models demonstrably capture, which remain unresolved, and which are not detected under qualified measurements and controls. The organising axis is known protein mechanisms and constraints, each treated as an independently gated assay. This document owns the map, its gates and each gate's admission condition, and it records no measurement of its own. It supersedes the earlier spine in which the axis was statistical information levels and one level had to be cleared before the next was asked; that spine is not retained beside this one.

*Unresolved* and *not detected* are different verdicts and are never merged. Unresolved means the gate's instrument, support or readout cannot decide the question on the material available. Not detected means a qualified measurement with qualified controls resolved the model-side quantity against zero or below it. A gate with no admissible control and a gate with a resolved negative are recorded differently, and neither is written as the other.

Two other documents keep what they already own. The [information-hierarchy protocol](D1_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY_PROTOCOL.md) owns the pairwise design — the target, the comparator chain, the label budget and the nuisance construction — and the estimand discipline every gate inherits: held-family phenotype prediction, sequence-model approximation and generation are three separate estimands and an answer to one is not an answer to another; a no-context model, an external-profile control and a supplied-homolog model are three regimes and not one comparison; and inside any one comparison the assay and variant support, label budgets, family splits, target transformations, fitting and tuning budget and evaluation weighting are matched, with external resources bound to source hashes. That document is a pre-registration whose results now exist, so it keeps its pre-data wording and is not edited by this revision. The [canonical audit](INTERPRETABILITY_TRANSFER_AUDIT.md) owns every finding, interval, retraction and limitation, so the statuses below point to its claim register (F18–F20) and its limitations catalogue (L47–L50) instead of restating them.

## What is a mechanism here, and what is only a control

Composition, k-mer and other local sequence statistics, independent-site profiles, coevolution estimates and homolog-retrieval support are **controls and competing explanatory classes, not biological mechanisms**. They do not appear as gates below. A control's job is to make a model-side quantity earn its increment; calling one of them a mechanism would license a capability claim about a descriptor rather than about a constraint that acts on a protein.

The failed local-pattern block is what this distinction looks like when it is ignored. That block was declared as the rung to be cleared at the "local" level, and it turned out to be a k-mer statistic that does not transfer across held-out wild-type clusters at all — it lowers the held-out correlation of the baseline it augments (L48). A statistic that cannot generalise across held-out clusters was never a mechanism in the first place, and clearing it would have licensed nothing. Local chemistry and local context remain a real mechanism gate; the k-mer block is one candidate control inside it, and a failed one. A qualified local control has since been built for that gate and the gate has reported (F21), which is what the distinction buys: the mechanism question survived its first control's failure, and the failure is retained as the reason the qualification condition exists rather than as the state of the gate.

Two rules follow and are programme-level rather than per-gate. None of composition, local sequence statistics, profile, retrieval, pairwise interaction or higher-order interaction is a mandatory ladder: they are competing explanations, tested independently wherever the support allows it, and a model-side quantity may be compared against any of them without first clearing another. And the crossed-control outcome recorded as F19 is a statement about controls rather than a mechanism verdict: composition does not account for the representation information in the two native protein arms, the mutation-local profile block is the binding control and absorbs the larger part of the increment, and a residual above zero at every seed survives it for two arms only. That is control qualification. Which mechanism, if any, the surviving residual belongs to is what the gates below ask.

## The standard sequence inside a gate

Every gate runs the same six steps in order, and reports at whatever step it has reached:

1. **Endpoint qualification.** The measured endpoint is qualified on its own, with no model quantity read against it, as the label instrument was for the residue-interaction gate.
2. **Baseline and control qualification.** Each control must be a competent predictor of held-out groups in its own right. A control that degrades the baseline it augments licenses nothing, whichever way the model-side increment then moves.
3. **Native model behaviour.** The model's own scores on its native interface, against those controls.
4. **Representation readout.** A fitted readout of frozen states, whose class and depth bound whatever it returns.
5. **Held-family and remote-family generalization.** The increment under family holdout, and then under remote-family, identity-band, retrieval-depth and near-duplicate stratification.
6. **Causal validation where justified.** Conditional on a resolved and generalising increment worth intervening on, not a routine final step. A gate may close before it, and most will.

A gate reports only what its own controls, endpoint and readout class can resolve, so a negative at one gate never determines another. Any gate's formulation — its endpoint, its controls, its statistical procedure or the gate itself — is revised, simplified or redesigned when results show the formulation inadequate, with the reason recorded in that gate's document. No experiment is forced toward a predetermined capability structure, and a redesign never converts an earlier negative into a positive or a positive into a negative.

**One programme-level sequencing rule cuts across those six steps: every representation-level verdict is provisional until the readout reassessment completes.** All qualification work — endpoints, baselines, controls and datasets — proceeds to completion in parallel, and model quantities may be extracted in advance; what waits is the verdict at step 4, not the measurement. Once the readout-class sweep and the full-depth re-extraction land, the representation analyses at every gate are systematically revisited and, where necessary, recomputed. This is why the readout reassessment is recorded below as measurement-validity work spanning every gate rather than as a gate of its own: it now has a scheduling consequence and not only a conceptual one, because its outcome determines what the representation half of every other gate means.

**Likelihood-level verdicts are not deferred, and that asymmetry is what makes the rule tractable.** A native likelihood enters a comparison as one scalar feature, so a linear readout of it is close to the full function space over that feature and the readout-class question barely bites. A 1,024-coordinate fixed projection of four pooled hidden-state summaries at two retained depths is one compressed choice among many, and it is there that a null may belong to the readout rather than to the states. So each gate's likelihood findings stand as recorded, and only its representation findings are provisional; a reader who treats the whole programme as provisional has merged the two halves.

## The ten mechanism gates

**Local chemistry and local context.** Does a frozen model carry information about how charge, hydrophobicity, local motifs and short-range structural preference constrain a substitution's effect, beyond controls built from the same local information? Admission condition, prespecified and binding: a local control may enter a capability comparison only if it raises, or at minimum does not lower, the held-out-cluster correlation of the baseline it augments, at every prespecified split seed. Priority high; **the gate has reported a verdict of the intended kind.** Six candidates were declared and digest-bound before any fit, two were discarded by the condition above, and the carried control raises both baselines at every seed with every contribution interval excluding zero, giving the strongest control set this programme has measured. A qualified local control does *not* absorb the model-side increment and absorbs a measurable part of it. The nine-arm primary analysis found a residual resolved at all three seeds in three arms and read that as an interface property; the 33-arm generalizability check **refuted that reading** and it is withdrawn — three protein-corpus arms emitting one token per residue do not survive, the eight panel survivors share something narrower than an interface class, and no account of it is asserted (F21). What generalises is the likelihood result, and it sharpens into a boundary drawn by pretraining corpus: over the strongest control measured, the native likelihood's increment resolves at all three seeds in 14 of 33 arms and those 14 are exactly the protein-pretrained checkpoints, while no text-corpus checkpoint reading protein reaches a resolved seed (F25). Segmentation is excluded more strongly than the nine-arm stratification could support: one token per residue is necessary in this panel and far from sufficient. **The two boundaries this gate drew are handed to Direction 2 by [D1_MATCHED_PAIR_HANDOFF.md](D1_MATCHED_PAIR_HANDOFF.md), which is the handoff of record**: it characterises the 33-arm panel against seventeen declared properties, finds the likelihood boundary perfectly separated by protein pretraining and the representation boundary separated by nothing except checkpoint lineage, ranks the crossings a mechanism study can act on, and states for each what it would and would not license. Two of its conclusions bind the work that follows. The representation boundary supports no well-matched pair, and transplant feasibility fails for all six candidate pairs as a matter of record, so transplant work stays on the ProLLaMA lineage with one recommended non-Llama replication on the likelihood side. And 8 survivors among 33 arms against 17 co-varying properties identifies nothing, so every separating property there is descriptive and confounded. One result of the gate is about the cohort rather than any model — a seven-residue-window predictor reaches about nine tenths of the composition control's held-cluster ranking, so neither that control nor the profile control is free of local information (F26). The earlier dipeptide-and-tripeptide block is superseded, and L48 is amended rather than retired, because that failure is why the condition exists. The extension of this measurement from the nine-arm panel to all 34 admitted arms is in progress, so the panel-wide figure is not settled. Record: [D1_GATE_LOCAL_CONTEXT.md](D1_GATE_LOCAL_CONTEXT.md).

**Folding and stability.** Does a frozen model carry information about how a sequence change affects stability and foldability, beyond sequence, profile and retrieval controls on the same support? Admission condition: the single-mutant stability endpoint is qualified in its own right — its own between-protease agreement and its own noise floor — before any model quantity is read against it, rather than inheriting the four-state cycle endpoint's admission. Priority high; **the gate has reported, with the likelihood half final and the representation half provisional.** On its own 101-group, 25,856-variant single-mutant cohort the likelihood increment over the qualified controls resolves above zero at all three split seeds for 4 of 33 arms and below zero for none, every one of the four a residue-level protein-pretrained checkpoint, and all 11 residue-level arms carry a positive three-seed mean against panel medians near zero for the BPE and byte-level strata (F28). Two bounds are part of what those numbers mean rather than caveats on them: the qualified control set holds exactly one block whose own contribution is resolved away from zero, so *beyond controls* is weaker here than the phrase suggests (L48), and the support conditions on wild-type resolvability in a way that is measured — the excluded backgrounds carry roughly thirteenfold the censored-row share of the retained ones — so the cohort represents resolvable stability states. It is distinct from the residue-interaction work by design and not a re-run of it: the first-order single-mutant likelihood signal that carried F20 was measured as a *control* inside an interaction experiment, on 8,192 four-state cycles confined to 217 site pairs the source study itself selected as suspected interactions, so it estimates nothing about stability prediction at large. On the single-mutant endpoint the support is far larger and is not selected for suspected coupling, which is where a stability claim has to be qualified. Record: [D1_GATE_STABILITY.md](D1_GATE_STABILITY.md); its endpoint, support, control verdicts and likelihood increments are stated as final and its representation increments as provisional, with the full-width states retained so the revisit is a refit rather than a re-extraction.

**Residue interactions.** On a measured four-state double-mutant cycle, does a frozen model quantity predict compensatory nonadditivity beyond additive, nonlinear-additive, sequence/profile, tokenisation and fitted pairwise-coupling controls? Admission condition: a qualified label instrument first, then a pairwise sequence comparator admitted by a label-independent held-out-homolog check, with every increment compared on identical support and never across supports. Priority high; settled, and *not detected* for the representation contrast on any arm, with the reproducible model-side signal sitting in single-mutant likelihood scoring instead (F20) — which is why the stability gate above exists. The comparator is admissible on 45 of the 64 held groups and absent on the other 19, where this gate is *unresolved* rather than negative and no weaker estimator is substituted. Its ceiling is constrained rather than merely unmeasured: the comparator's couplings are prior-dominated at the retained alignment depth, and that depth cannot be increased from the corpora held on this host (audit §0, L49). Record: [D1_PAIRWISE_EPISTASIS_RESULTS.md](D1_PAIRWISE_EPISTASIS_RESULTS.md), with [D1_PAIRWISE_LABEL_INSTRUMENT.md](D1_PAIRWISE_LABEL_INSTRUMENT.md) for the instrument and [D1_PAIRWISE_BASELINE_READINESS.md](D1_PAIRWISE_BASELINE_READINESS.md) for the grouping contract, cohort and comparator.

**Higher-order dependence.** Does a frozen model quantity carry information about multi-residue or background-dependent effects that are not reducible to pairwise terms? Admission condition: a bounded pilot that qualifies its own endpoint and support before any model-side reading, and that does not require the residue-interaction gate to have resolved positive. Higher-order background dependence is not nested inside the four-state contrast — a cycle over two substitutions cannot express a dependence on a third and fourth — so a pairwise negative neither predicts nor forecloses it, and conditioning the pilot on pairwise positivity would reimpose exactly the ladder this plan removes. Priority high as a pilot; **the gate has now reported twice and is unresolved on measured grounds rather than for want of data.** The MegaScale pilot stopped at step 1 because those bytes contain no measured combinatorial states at all — a zero since shown to be mechanical, every state of order 3 or above there being a length-changing construct seen through a truncated column. The gate was then revived on ProteinGym, where such states exist in quantity, a genuine replicate channel was sourced for the richest position support, and the order-3 endpoint fails its own floor at **essentially the pairwise cohort's effective support**: the reproducible third-order component sits at about a quarter of the labels' own channel noise, while the same assay's order-2 component is resolved above zero and the admitted pairwise endpoint's ratio is nearly three. So the failure is an order property of well-powered labels, not a power failure, and still **unresolved and not *not detected***: no model quantity was read, the 33-arm roster was not run and stays unconditioned, and the GPU budget was not spent. F23 carries the quantities. Records: [D1_GATE_HIGHER_ORDER.md](D1_GATE_HIGHER_ORDER.md) for the MegaScale pilot and [D1_GATE_HIGHER_ORDER_EXTENDED.md](D1_GATE_HIGHER_ORDER_EXTENDED.md) for the ProteinGym expansion and the refusal of its second candidate.

**3D structural constraints.** Do model-side signals concentrate where spatial contact, packing and long-range geometry predict they should — in contacting residue pairs relative to matched non-contact pairs? Admission condition: the measured endpoint and the contact assignment are both qualified before any model-side claim, and the existing sequence-separation strata are not a contact contrast and may not be reinterpreted as one. Any quantity taken from the folding predictor also carries that instrument's licensed scope, which is a paired within-sequence contrast with calibration attained on 2 of the 19 arms that report one and inherited by the other 17 ([D1_STRUCTURE_INSTRUMENT_RECONCILIATION.md](D1_STRUCTURE_INSTRUMENT_RECONCILIATION.md), audit §0). Priority high; **the gate has reported, and it stopped at step 1.** Part 1 asks whether the *measured* cycle endpoint concentrates at contacts, as a biological-validity check on the admitted label instrument, and part 2 was to be conditional on it. Part 1 is not passed: on the declared primary contact definition, matching and weighting the measured contact-minus-matched-control difference does not resolve on the raw endpoint and is bounded near zero on the pair-specific endpoint that removes the global response, so a model-side contact enrichment measured on that endpoint would not have been interpretable in either direction, and part 2 was not run rather than run and reported as uninformative. This is *unresolved* for the mechanism question on this support, not *not detected* for any model: **no model-side contact number exists for any arm**, because part 2 was never run rather than run and found null. Three elements make the stop informative rather than bare. A pure-noise negative control on the same support, built from the two protease channels' disagreement, shows how much apparent enrichment is reachable from label-instrument discordance alone, and the adjusted interaction estimate sits inside that band. A structure positive control resolves on the same annotation, alignment and chain, so the structures, the numbering and the accessibility calculation carry real information about this assay and the flat contrast is a property of the contrast rather than of the instrument. And what raw difference exists behaves like the global stability response: contacts carry larger single-mutant effects, and removing the global response removes the difference. The provenance is also disjoint from the predicted-structure instrument — all 64 backgrounds resolved to experimental entries at 100.0% wild-type identity with no folding anywhere in the gate — so the narrowed pLDDT scope does not reach it. What fails is the contrast, on a support whose site pairs the source study selected as coupling tables and whose control arm carries a Kish effective 25.1 site pairs. F22 carries the quantities. Record: [D1_GATE_STRUCTURE_CONTACT.md](D1_GATE_STRUCTURE_CONTACT.md).

**Conformational dynamics and allostery.** Does a frozen model carry information about state transitions and long-range regulatory coupling — the constraints that act on a protein's motion rather than on one static state? Admission condition: the first two steps of the standard sequence, a qualified dynamics or allostery endpoint and controls that are competent on held-out groups, before any model-side reading. Priority later stage; not launched, and no record exists yet. It is opened early only if existing data make a qualified endpoint immediately tractable.

**Molecular recognition and function.** Does a frozen model carry information about binding, catalysis and substrate specificity beyond family membership and retrieval support? Admission condition: the same first two steps, with the further requirement that a functional endpoint be a measurement rather than an annotation — a profile or family call is not a functional outcome. Priority later stage; not launched, and no record exists yet.

**Cellular regulation and environment.** Does a frozen model carry information about expression, localisation, degradation, post-translational modification and membrane context — constraints that act on a protein in a cell rather than on an isolated sequence? Admission condition: the same first two steps, on an endpoint whose measured quantity is attributable to the protein rather than to the assay system around it. Priority later stage; not launched, and no record exists yet.

**Evolution and adaptation.** Does a frozen model provide information *beyond* profile statistics, coevolution estimates, homolog retrieval and memorization? The question is deliberately framed as exceeding those controls, because they are the competing explanations and not mechanisms in their own right. Admission condition: the strata are declared before the stratified reading, with unassigned, truncated and no-hit cases retained in every denominator; no detected alignment in a searched snapshot is not absence from pretraining, so this gate reports a stratified dependence and never a certified exclusion. Its declared strata include a source-provenance stratum: the profile-residual gain recomputed with the Tsuboyama 2023 assays excluded, the overlap being the one quantified in the forward scope below. Priority high; **answered for the likelihood half, deferred for the representation half.** The gate refits nothing — a stratum's estimate is the same paired statistic over the same fitted held-out predictions on the units that stratum holds, so the whole-support estimate decomposes into its strata exactly. The first-order likelihood gain on measured stability **survives** every axis with the units to resolve, and its gradient runs opposite to a retrieval explanation: the point estimates are larger where no near-duplicate is retrieved and larger where alignment depth is shallow, and panel-wide the shallowest depth band carries twice as many arms above zero as the whole support. That half is likelihood-only and therefore final, and F24 carries it. The representation half is computed and **provisional** under the sequencing rule, with one distinction that must survive any later reading: on the 23 clusters with no retrieved near-duplicate the residual is unresolved for want of support rather than vanishing, its point estimates sitting at 52% to 103% of the whole-anchor value while the interval more than doubles. The source-provenance stratum also answered: the increment is 2.4 to 3.3 times larger on the clusters sharing the stability cohort's source and survives their removal at a narrower interval (L50). **The binding limitation is that both cohorts are saturated with high-identity units** — 49 of 64 stability groups and 140 of 163 anchor clusters at or above 95% identity — so the remote-identity end is unresolved in both directions and neither gain has been tested against remote homology; a support with 105 entirely remote groups has been measured as a route, at a label floor about two thirds of that endpoint's own spread. Record: [D1_GATE_RETRIEVAL_MEMORIZATION.md](D1_GATE_RETRIEVAL_MEMORIZATION.md).

**Generation and control.** Do the constraints a model has learned yield generated sequences that satisfy stated biological requirements? This is an independent capability axis and not another scoring assay: scoring, generation and context utilisation have already been shown not to collapse onto one axis, a cross-checkpoint positive association coexisting with a within-lineage counterexample (audit §0), so a generation result neither confirms nor refutes a scoring result. Admission condition: all attempts, complete products, censored prefixes, failures, exact duplicates and near-duplicate groups stay in every denominator, and post-hoc assignment of a generated sequence to a family is a selection step whose unassigned outcomes are reported rather than dropped. Priority high; **the gate has reported on all three of its endpoints, two with a verdict and one without.** Seven generators were built and qualified against the statistics each declares before any recognition rate was read, and the comparison is paired on the attempt ledger's own denominator. Four of the five qualified controls reach a curated family at most once in 16,000 attempts, so the binding control is a contiguous corpus fragment at the attempt's own length, and against it 1 of 20 cells clears every qualified generator on any curated family and 3 of 20 on a complete domain (F27). The structure endpoint has no verdict and the reason is the instrument: per-arm natural-versus-shuffle separation is unlicensed on 17 of the 19 contrast-reporting arms, so no structure cell contributes to either endpoint verdict. Assignment to the requested class stays where it already is, because no matched statistical generator carries a class label and the informative control there is a label-carrying one, recorded as a recommendation rather than built. Record: [D1_GATE_GENERATIVE_CONTROL.md](D1_GATE_GENERATIVE_CONTROL.md).

## Two activities that are not mechanism gates

**Global but additive context: control qualification.** Is residual signal explained by longer-range sequence context that is still additive across positions, rather than by any interaction between positions? This is a control to be qualified, not a mechanism to be claimed, and it sits here rather than among the ten for that reason. The additive long-range control must itself be competent on held-out groups under the rule that binds every control, and the contrast must separate an additive long-range explanation from an interaction explanation on the same support rather than by argument. **Answered, and the answer is that it absorbed nothing.** This was the primary alternative explanation for the first-order likelihood gain recorded in F20, which is as large or larger on the distant-separation stratum: a quantity that grows with sequence separation is what an additive long-range dependence predicts as readily as an interaction does. It does not produce it here — a declared long-range additive block absorbs between nothing and about four percent of that gain, on the full support and on the distant stratum, leaving the panel count unchanged, and the bounded-receptive-field comparator absorbs the same negligible share, so the information is not local either. Because the gate is declared over model likelihood only and measured no representation contrast, it is a **standing result and carries no provisional label**. Its own bound travels with it and has L48's shape: the declared block's contribution does not resolve above zero at all three seeds, so the gate shows that the implemented alternative absorbed nothing rather than that long-range additivity is excluded, and a stronger version would need a richer representation of distant content, a nonlinear per-position response, or more effective site pairs. Record: [D1_GATE_GLOBAL_CONTEXT.md](D1_GATE_GLOBAL_CONTEXT.md); F20's status carries the consequence for the pairwise row.

**Readout reassessment: measurement validity across every gate.** Are the current representation-side nulls a measurement boundary of the fitted readout or a boundary of the representation? This is not a gate but a validity question whose outcome conditions every gate that reads a representation, which is why it is not listed among the ten and why no gate's negative is final while it is open. The condition is that one factor vary at a time on the same frozen states — depth across the full stack, compressed against uncompressed features, linear against controlled nonlinear heads — with the cohort, the per-panel supports, the split seeds, the fold maps, the targets, the weighting and the resampling contract held fixed, and with the admitted class reproduced in every cell before any new class is read. Because every representation-level verdict waits on it, this work sets the programme's release order and not only its interpretation. Two experiments are in flight, and both records now exist with their results sections explicitly pending: a [readout-class sweep](D1_READOUT_CLASS_SWEEP.md) varying the fitted function on the retained states, and a [depth sweep](D1_READOUT_DEPTH_SWEEP.md) that re-extracts every block to lift the two-depth bound the class sweep cannot pass. Both bear directly on F18 and F20, because each of those negatives currently bounds a compressed linear readout at two extraction depths rather than the representations, and neither may be pre-empted.

## Priority ordering

Immediately testable, and where work is placed now: local chemistry and local context, folding and stability, evolution and adaptation against its profile and retrieval controls, the global-context control qualification where a gate needs it, residue interactions, and the structure-conditioned contrast. Higher-order dependence was in this tier as a pilot and has left it by returning unresolvable at endpoint qualification, which is the ordering rule working rather than a gate being skipped. Later stage: conformational dynamics, allostery, catalysis, binding and cellular context — each opened earlier only if existing data make a qualified endpoint immediately tractable, and not opened merely to complete the map.

## Forward scope

Three items sit beyond the gates and are recorded here so that they are not rediscovered later as new directions.

Endpoints beyond the main deep-mutational-scanning axis are deliberately few, and the one already in place is not a source-independent replication. Measured stability serves as the confirmation endpoint for the mutation-ranking axis, on a different assay technology and a different measured quantity, but the two axes draw on one experimental source. Measured directly from the frozen readout cohort (digest `4093ac34368cd9e7be02e5c84a22655ce78cb9bb4e4d58d626f281868dbe7992`), 64 of its 217 assays are Tsuboyama 2023 — 29.5% — for example `AMFR_HUMAN_Tsuboyama_2023_4G3O`, `ARGR_ECOLI_Tsuboyama_2023_1AOY` and `BBC1_YEAST_Tsuboyama_2023_1TG0`; all 64 survive into the 201-assay anchor on which the readout and crossed-control findings rest, where they are 64 of 201 assays and 8,192 of 25,728 variants (31.8% of each) and fall in 64 of the 163 wild-type-family resampling units that carry the uncertainty (39.3%), two of those units also holding a non-Tsuboyama assay. That is the same source supplying the entire MegaScale stability cohort behind the residue-interaction gate and the newly launched stability gate, so on this panel the shared source component is close to a third of the mutation-ranking axis rather than a marginal technicality. A source-independent sensitivity must therefore identify and account for that overlap instead of treating stability as a second independent biological confirmation; the audit's catalogue carries the share and the constraint it puts on reading F19 and F20 together as L50. The evolution-and-adaptation gate has been asked to report the profile-residual gain with those 64 assays excluded as a declared source-provenance stratum, and that gate's record will carry the estimate. At most one further high-quality endpoint — an activity or a binding measurement — is added, and only if the emerging map needs it to separate two explanations the existing endpoints cannot.

Final external confirmation applies to the strongest two or three findings of the map, on independent families or datasets not used during development. "Not used during development" is to be read literally and is stated here so that it is not reinterpreted later: the confirmation data must come from neither ProteinGym's existing panel nor Tsuboyama 2023, because those two supply both axes of the current map. The effect, the paired baseline, the meaningful margin, the family weighting, the multiplicity handling and the family count are fixed before any confirmation outcome is read. A development-set outcome is not restated as a confirmation, and an interval covering zero there is unresolved rather than equivalent.

Strengthening the pairwise baseline is limited by retained local resources rather than by effort. Only a search index of the large reference corpus is held rather than its sequences, and the one complete sequence corpus available locally is far smaller, so no deeper alignment can be built offline. That is why the residue-interaction gate's ceiling is recorded as constrained, and why a deeper coupling estimate is a data-access question rather than a scheduling one.

## What this document is not

It records no measurement. Every quantity, interval and limitation behind a status above belongs to the audit or to that gate's own results document, and a status here is a pointer rather than an independent assertion. A gate marked settled is settled on its own support, under its own controls and its own readout class; none of the gates identifies a mechanism by itself, a resolved increment is accessible predictive information and not a demonstrated biological computation, and no gate outcome is evidence of encoded biological knowledge. The gate documents named as absent are named from their gate's question and from this plan alone; no content of theirs is described here, because none has been read.
