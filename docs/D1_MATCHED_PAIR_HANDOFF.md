# Matched checkpoint pairs for a mechanism study of the readout boundary

**Design and characterisation, 2026-09-24. No measurement of a model quantity is made here.** The [local-context gate](D1_GATE_LOCAL_CONTEXT.md) closed with two outcomes on one 33-arm panel that do not line up. Over the strongest control the programme has measured — composition, mutation-local profile statistics and the qualified local-chemistry block `wall` together, at +0.47906 to +0.48290 held-cluster Spearman — the native likelihood's increment resolves above zero at all three split seeds in 14 of 33 arms, and those 14 are exactly the protein-pretrained checkpoints. The representation increment over the same control resolves at all three seeds in 8 arms, and the gate asserts no account of what those 8 share. This document turns that asymmetry into a short list of checkpoint pairs a Direction-2 study can act on, and states for each pair what it would and would not license.

It owns the pair construction and the handoff specification. The gate owns the increments and their intervals; the [readout report](D1_READOUT_RESULTS.md) owns the panel, the strata and the native-likelihood values; the [depth sweep](D1_READOUT_DEPTH_SWEEP.md) owns the per-block design this composes with; the [ProLLaMA transplant](D2_PROLLAMA_CAPABILITY_LOCALIZATION.md) owns the one lineage already being used this way. Nothing here re-states an increment those documents carry.

Two scope statements bind everything below. The representation side of the panel is **provisional** pending the programme's readout-class reassessment, so the survival pattern the pairs are built against may change under recomputation; the section on recomputation says which pairs survive that and which do not. And a separating property found among 33 arms and 8 survivors is **descriptive and confounded**, for reasons the next two sections make quantitative.

## The declared property set, frozen before it was related to any outcome

Seventeen properties were assembled from declared checkpoint metadata alone, digest-bound, and only then related to the survival pattern. The table is `logs/matched_pair_handoff/arm_properties.json`, SHA-256 `da9009db8bff12b51f35577dd93e27a838ab250b3ba2c81e436d160ee477385b`, built by `scripts/transfer/assemble_matched_pair_properties.py properties`. Fifteen properties describe the released weights; two describe how the admitted extraction read them and are carried separately, because both turn out to be material confounds.

| Group | Properties |
| --- | --- |
| Checkpoint | depth in transformer blocks; hidden width; nominal parameter count; released storage precision; architecture family; attention structure; MLP structure; tokenizer family; scored-target alphabet; measured segmentation; pretraining corpus; whether any declared training stage saw protein sequence; training objective; instruction or conditioning stage; protein training route |
| Execution | precision the admitted extraction loaded; batch size it ran |

Every value is read from an artifact, read from `src/transfer/arms.py`, or transcribed from a named repository record. Depth comes from the admitted expansion archive's 34 per-arm manifests, as the last captured block index plus one; hidden width is transcribed from the admitted readout report's two per-arm width tables. Where `arms.py` also declares depth or width the assembler requires the two to agree and raises otherwise, which is what makes a transcription checkable rather than a second source of truth. A property no record states is written `not_recorded` rather than guessed: attention structure is unrecorded for 8 of the 33 anchor arms, MLP structure for 2, and released storage precision for 28, and the stored-byte totals in the manifests include container headers, so a parameter count derived from them is approximate rather than exact.

Three consequences of that follow the table into every comparison. Nominal parameter counts are figures at different roundings, so two arms count as matching when theirs agree to within 10% and otherwise as differing; every other property is compared exactly. A property unrecorded on either side of a pair is classed **undecidable** rather than differing, because ranking pairs by how little the repository happens to know about them would put the least documented pair first. And two arms declared as protein decoders whose corpus identity is the `undeclared` sentinel — ProGen3-112M and ProtGPT3-1.3B — are recorded as protein-pretrained with an unrecorded corpus; one of the two is a survivor and one is not, so that assignment cannot bias the boundary in either direction.

The one arm outside the anchor roster, ZymCTRL, is assembled and then excluded by name: its EC-conditioned 40-assay support at 30 resampling units is not the anchor's 201 assays at 163, and no increment of its is comparable with an anchor arm's.

## What separates the two boundaries, and what does not

`separate` verifies the digest above before reading any outcome class. Its report is `logs/matched_pair_handoff/separation.json`, SHA-256 `e3b3df9f78f32ec358bf5a907d53052652ff6f15466988be29aeb05324d8626c`.

**The likelihood boundary is separated by one property, and the gate already reports it as final.** `protein_pretraining` holds for exactly 14 of the 33 arms and the likelihood increment over C+P+`wall` resolves above zero at all three seeds in exactly those 14 — perfect separation, 14 of 14 inside against 0 of 19 outside. `protein_training_route` separates identically because it is the same fact in another spelling: from scratch or continued from a text parent inside, none outside. No other declared property separates that boundary.

**The representation boundary is separated by no property except checkpoint lineage.** Of the 17, only `architecture_family` has no value on both sides, and its values inside are `progen` (5), `progen3` (2) and `proteinglm` (1) against eight other families outside — which is the gate's own statement that the survivors are the ProGen2, ProGen3 and ProteinGLM checkpoints specifically, restated as a field. It is a lineage label, not a mechanism, and treating it as an explanation would be circular.

Five properties are constant across the 8 survivors and not across the other 25, so each is necessary in this panel and none is sufficient:

| Property | Survivors | The other 25 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `protein_pretraining` | yes, 8 of 8 | yes for 6, no for 19 |
| `tokenizer_family` | `residue`, 8 of 8 | `residue` for 2, six other families for 23 |
| `measured_segmentation` | one token per residue, 8 of 8 | one token per residue for 10, multi-residue for 15 |
| `instruction_or_conditioning_stage` | none, 8 of 8 | none for 22, instruction-tuned for 3 |
| `protein_training_route` | from scratch, 8 of 8 | from scratch for 3, from a text parent for 3, none for 19 |

Their conjunction holds for 10 of the 33 arms and 8 of those 10 survive. The two exceptions are **ProtGPT3-1.3B** and **RITA-XL**, and they are why no account of the pattern can be read off this table. `training_objective` is `next_token_likelihood` for all 33 arms, so the objective axis has zero variance on this panel and no objective claim is testable here at all.

No ordinal property separates, and none carries a direction. Depth runs 10 to 36 blocks among survivors against 4 to 64 outside; hidden width 384 to 4096 against 768 to 7168; nominal parameter count 112M to 7.25e9 against 73M to 32.5e9; scored-target alphabet 32 to 134 symbols against 26 to 152,064. Every range overlaps. The ProGen2 ladder settles the direction question inside one lineage: all five rungs survive at all three seeds across depths 12, 27, 27, 32 and 32, widths 1024 to 4096, nominal 151.1M to 6.4436e9, two storage precisions (4.00 stored bytes per nominal parameter for small, medium and base against 2.00 for large and xlarge) and two declared corpora, while the panel's deepest and widest non-survivors — Galactica-30B at 48 blocks of width 7168 and Qwen2.5-32B at 64 of width 5120 — resolve nothing. Scale, depth, width and storage precision are therefore not the axis.

Two properties carry confounds worth naming rather than burying. Extraction load precision is `float32` for 31 of the 33 arms and `bfloat16` for exactly two, the two ProGen3 arms, both survivors — so any ProGen3-specific residual is confounded with the one precision deviation in the panel, and both ProGen3 arms additionally carry the singleton-only batching bound the readout report records. Extraction batch size is 1 for ProGen2 and the ProGen3 arms and 8 for ProteinGLM, so the survivors span both batch regimes and the batch-one requalification's structural vacuity applies unevenly inside the surviving set.

**The confounding statement, stated once and meant for every number above.** With 8 survivors among 33 arms and 17 properties, the panel cannot identify which property matters, and several of them co-vary across these checkpoints by construction: a residue tokenizer, one token per residue, a protein corpus and training from scratch are four cells of one release decision in every arm that has them, and checkpoint lineages inside the panel are correlated. Nothing above is a cause. Each row is a count over an unmatched roster, uncorrected across properties, arms, endpoints or seeds.

**Rank 6, Galactica-6.7B against ProGen2-xlarge**, is the only pair in the panel that is depth-matched at 32 blocks, width-matched at 4096, position-resolved on both sides and crosses the representation survival boundary. Two further pairs are **controls rather than targets**, and both are cheap.

**ProGen2-base against ProGen2-medium** is the panel's only crossing-free matched pair: one declared property differs (pretraining corpus), one is undecidable (released storage precision, unrecorded on both sides), thirteen match, and the two store byte-identical tensor totals of 3,059,237,976 bytes. Neither endpoint's class differs. It is the floor control for any corpus claim made inside one protein lineage: changing the corpus mixture at fixed architecture, scale, tokenizer and precision moves neither outcome's class.

**The ProGen2 ladder as a whole** is the panel's only well-matched invariance result on the representation side, and it bounds what any explanation of the residual may appeal to. Five rungs, one architecture, one 31-symbol residue tokenizer behind a 32-column head, 151.1M to 6.4436e9 nominal parameters, depths 12 to 32, widths 1024 to 4096, two storage precisions, two corpora, all eight-of-eight resolved. A candidate mechanism that predicts a residual only at one scale, one depth, one width or one precision is already refuted by it.

## What each pair would and would not license

The general statement first, because it applies to every row above. **A pair differing in several declared properties cannot attribute an effect to any one of them**, and a pair differing in one still confounds that property with everything undeclared about the two training runs — the data order, the schedule, the tokenizer's learned merges, the number of tokens seen, and every choice no model card states. The ProLLaMA design already records this for its own three-cell difference: continued pretraining there fixes a corpus, a schedule and a data order at once, so a difference across that step prices the released adaptation as a whole.

Per pair:

**Rank 1 (ProLLaMA)** licenses a parameter-group localisation of the native likelihood's mutation-effect ranking, which is what it is being used for. It licenses nothing about the representation residual: neither stage nor the parent has a representation increment resolved above zero over C+P+`wall` at any seed, so there is no residual there to localise. It also supplies no residue-indexed intervention site, because the lineage's measured segmentation is 1.3043 to 1.7917 residues per token and it is one of the 15 arms for which no position-resolved depth summary is defined.

**Rank 2 (GPT-2-large / ProtGPT2)** licenses a matched-scale, matched-architecture behavioural and representational comparison of a protein corpus against a text corpus. It does **not** license a partial transplant, and this is the pair's decisive limitation: the two vocabularies share a size of 50,257 pieces while their pieces are learned over different corpora, so one id does not denote the same piece in both, installing one checkpoint's embedding into the other is shape-valid and semantically empty, and every cell that crosses the embedding boundary mixes two token-id semantics while the floor and ceiling remain exact. Any transplant here is confined to body groups read with the recipient's own tokenizer, and the result is a parameter-space contrast rather than a capability transfer. It also licenses no residue-indexed patching site, for the same reason as rank 1, and it matches only on the two-level segmentation *class*: the measured rate is 2.5714 to 4.9800 residues per token for ProtGPT2 and is not recorded for GPT-2-large on a literal amino-acid string, so the match is coarser than the column suggests. ProtGPT2's likelihood is the native formatted-target quantity and may include predicted formatting tokens, which bounds any statement about its side.

**Rank 3 (ProGen3-112M / ProtGPT3-1.3B)** licenses the statement that two protein decoders sharing a residue tokenizer, one token per residue, sparse mixture-of-experts feed-forward blocks, an unrecorded corpus and a resolved likelihood increment differ in the representation residual. It separates none of depth, width, scale and architecture family, all four of which differ, and its ProGen3 side carries the panel's only bfloat16 extraction and the singleton-only batching bound, so any difference it shows is confounded with both. No transplant is defined: the architecture families differ, so tensor names do not correspond.

**Rank 4 (ProGen3-3B / RITA-XL)** licenses an equal-depth, block-index-to-block-index comparison at 24 blocks. Its outcome difference is one seed, so it licenses no verdict on its own and should not be a primary target.

**Rank 5 (Galactica-1.3B / InstructProtein)** licenses the statement that, at fixed depth, width, architecture family and segmentation, the release that added protein pretraining placed the native likelihood's mutation-effect ranking at +0.28372 [+0.25509, +0.31163] against +0.04754 [+0.02567, +0.06915] while leaving the representation increment in the unresolved class on both sides. The 0.236 gap between those point estimates is not a fitted paired contrast and carries no interval of its own. It does **not** attribute that to the corpus: the same release extended the vocabulary with twenty dedicated residue ids and added an instruction-tuning stage, so three declared axes move together. Whether the two checkpoints share an initialisation is **not declared anywhere in this repository**, so whether a transplant is even meaningful is an open question a census decides; if they do not share one, the pair is an architecture-matched comparison and no transplant is licensed at all.

**Rank 6 (Galactica-6.7B / ProGen2-xlarge)** licenses a depth-resolved comparison of where in 32 blocks each arm's increment sits, at matched width and matched segmentation, with no rescaling convention needed. It licenses no attribution to corpus, tokenizer or architecture, all three of which differ, and no transplant, because the architecture families differ. A cross-family activation transplant between two 4096-dimensional spaces would require a learned mapping, which is a new instrument with its own qualification and not a patching experiment.

**The recommendation, weighing the ranking against what it licenses.** The mechanism study should keep the likelihood boundary on the ProLLaMA lineage and add **rank 5 as its one non-Llama replication**, because rank 5 is the only pair that crosses the corpus boundary at matched depth, width and architecture family with one token per residue on both sides and position-resolved depth summaries available — exactly the three axes on which the ProLLaMA lineage cannot report. For the representation boundary the honest finding is that **the panel supports no well-matched pair**: the best of the 32 candidates that hold the likelihood class fixed differs in five declared properties with two undecidable, and the best that is depth- and width-matched crosses the likelihood boundary as well. The representation residual should therefore be attacked first by the depth sweep and by the within-ProGen2 invariance above, not by a cross-checkpoint transplant, and ranks 3, 4 and 6 should be entered only as depth-profile comparisons whose confounds are stated with every number.

## The handoff, specified so it needs no redesign

**The endpoint is unchanged and is the same one for every pair.** For a representation cell it is the paired increment in within-assay Spearman of the fitted predictor over the matched control, on the frozen 201-assay / 163-cluster / 25,728-variant anchor with cohort SHA-256 `4093ac34368cd9e7be02e5c84a22655ce78cb9bb4e4d58d626f281868dbe7992`; five deterministic outer and four inner wild-type-cluster folds at split seeds 20260923, 20260924 and 20260925, the inner seed being the outer seed plus 100 plus the zero-based outer-fold index; within-assay standardized average-rank targets; equal weight per wild-type cluster, per assay within a cluster and per variant within an assay; training-only weighted feature scaling; the admitted five-point ridge grid with ties broken toward the stronger penalty; the fixed representation-projection seeds 20260923 through 20260926; and 2,000 paired bootstrap draws at seed 20260923 over the unit *wild-type family at 50% identity*. The binding control is C+P+`wall` at +0.47955, +0.47906 and +0.48290 over the three seeds. For a likelihood cell it is the same anchor and the same resampling unit with the model entering as one scalar column, and raw likelihood ranking needs no folds, no penalty grid and no projection at all — which is why the ProLLaMA transplant runs that endpoint and nothing else.

**The retained artifacts that make this cheap** already exist and are not regenerated:

- The full-width per-state block outputs of the admitted readout extractions, retained on the cluster beside the projections, so a different projection width, a different depth or a nonlinear head replays as a fit with no model inference.
- The 99 local-context cell reports, each carrying its full fold map with outer and inner group membership, the selected ridge penalty per fold and design, the split and projection seeds, the per-assay metrics and the prediction digests, under `results/external_baseline/20260923223301_305478e124be/lcgm_*` and `.../20260923233257_e429ce7f31e4/lcgp_*`, with the fitted predictions beside each report as an NPZ.
- The frozen local-block declaration, SHA-256 `255b7e3d1e42893e9c05cd201e2ad9685015a24bc14b5aeefbdee102e5aa25c1`, in `src/transfer/local_context.py`, from which `wall` reproduces deterministically from the cohort with no projection and no random number; the outcome-blind qualification receipt, SHA-256 `e0fa349e3efc9ad7e21a637143103581fb5becd603509b9a7b24a67347931dd4`, which must not be re-qualified against a wider panel; and the two cross-cell admissions, SHA-256 `f6ca1dffba4a135a5955c79582994c6c16a4a2209a766e5748493908cd4ebade` and `6d18d2f40c99e6c969121d0806794c8dd4017af4ef3bc344093e7014b160447e`.
- The admitted expansion roster `expected.json`, SHA-256 `1bf26a6ae8d71e3c0f790f12c92da7d3dd4a9add7be6f7ef807836297e12dd0c`, and the final admission receipt, SHA-256 `763e7de57b625d55b88bbbe87ad5a68b226693a79fb5d1b5970505aeb6b9f695`, which bind supports, folds and matched-baseline agreement for every arm a pair names.
- The depth sweep's per-block archives once it lands, each carrying the native likelihood, the wild-type state, the ordered mutation identifiers, the measured effects, the profile scores and the batch repeat-check quantities under one bound measurement identity.

**Architectural compatibility differs by pair and decides what is even possible.** A transplant requires corresponding tensor names, corresponding shapes and a partition that covers the checkpoint. Only two of the six pairs could satisfy that, and neither satisfies it as a matter of record:

- Rank 5 shares a declared depth, width and model type, so every block's projection tensors are shape-compatible **if and only if** the head counts agree, which no record on this host states. The embedding and the LM head are not shape-compatible at all, 50,000 rows against 50,304, so any partition must place embedding and head in their own groups and a census that finds a shape mismatch must refuse rather than resize. The first step is therefore a tensor-name, shape and value census over the two checkpoints, on the model of the ProLLaMA census — which is CPU-only, needs no cohort and no forward pass, and reports element-level agreement per group as that census did (98.905% of elements differing whole-checkpoint, with every RMSNorm weight and every stored rotary frequency byte-identical). If it finds no shared initialisation, the pair is a comparison and not a transplant, and that is a result worth recording.
- Rank 2 shares depth, width, parameter count, architecture family and vocabulary size, so its bodies are shape-compatible; its vocabularies share no content, which is the limitation stated above.
- Ranks 3, 4 and 6 span architecture families, so no tensor correspondence exists and no transplant is defined. They are representation-level and behavioural comparisons only.

**The ceiling-and-floor controls a cell must pass before any localisation number is computed** are carried unchanged from the ProLLaMA design, which is the second reason these pairs are cheap:

1. **Floor.** Selecting no group leaves the recipient bit-for-bit and must reproduce its admitted endpoint inside its admitted interval.
2. **Ceiling.** Selecting every group reconstructs the donor bit-for-bit and must reproduce the donor's admitted endpoint inside its admitted interval. A recovered fraction read against a scale that does not reconstruct the admitted models means nothing, so the analysis refuses to compute one if either endpoint falls outside its interval.
3. **Partition completeness.** Every tensor of the checkpoint belongs to exactly one group, checked rather than assumed; that check is what makes the floor and the ceiling exact.
4. **Byte-identity refusal.** A group whose tensors are byte-identical across the two checkpoints is an algebraic no-op and must not be run, and a cell whose skipped tensors were not identical must refuse. A zero contrast from an identity is not evidence that the group is insensitive.
5. **Per-cell provenance.** After the copy, every live tensor digested off the loaded model must match the census digest of the checkpoint its group selects.
6. **Support replay.** Each cell replays the eligibility rule from the cohort and refuses unless the result matches the admitted extraction assay for assay, including every family label, wild-type identifier, mutation digest and packed-token count.
7. **Pipeline identity, for any representation cell.** Refitting the admitted class from the admitted retained states through the new code at the primary split seed must agree with the admitted report to at most 1e-8 absolute on the held-out predictions of B, R and B+R, with identical support, identical outer and inner fold membership and identical selected penalties.

## How this composes with the depth sweep

The per-layer sweep across all 1,004 blocks of all 34 arms reports, per arm, the increment over the matched baseline at each of its blocks, at every declared split seed on the primary `depth` axis, and — for the 19 arms whose rendering assigns exactly one token per residue — the position-resolved `mut` and `suffix` summaries as well. Do not wait for it. The composition rule is declarable now and does not depend on which way it comes out.

**A pair is a sharper patching target when three conditions hold together.** Both sides are among the 19 position-resolved arms, so a mutant-minus-wild-type difference at a residue index is defined on both and a residue-indexed intervention site exists. Their depths are equal, so block index *j* on one side corresponds to block index *j* on the other without a rescaling convention. And their per-block increment profiles differ at an identifiable block or contiguous band rather than only in the pooled two-depth endpoint. Under all three the intervention has a declared site — that block, those positions — and a declared prediction, that the increment at that block moves toward the donor's. A pooled endpoint supplies neither, which is why **a pair whose outcomes differ and whose depth profiles differ at an identifiable block is a far sharper target than a pair differing only in the endpoint**.

Two degradations follow, and both are stated so that a later reader does not repair them by convention. Where depths differ, any block correspondence is a convention rather than a measurement: the comparison is then between normalised depths *j*/(*L*−1) and the site is a matched band rather than a matched index, and the convention must be recorded with the number. Where either side is among the 15 multi-residue arms, no position-resolved summary exists and none is defined, so the pair supports whole-block or parameter-group interventions only and no residue-indexed site at all.

Applied to the six pairs, the rule gives a priority that does not match the raw ranking. Ranks 5 and 6 satisfy all three conditions subject to the profiles differing, at depths 24 and 32 respectively. Rank 4 satisfies them at depth 24 with a one-seed outcome difference. Rank 3 is position-resolved but depth-unequal, 10 against 17, so it degrades to a normalised-depth band. Ranks 1 and 2 fail the first condition outright: both sides of each are multi-residue, so neither supplies a residue-indexed site however the sweep comes out. **Rank 6 is the only pair in the panel that is depth-matched, width-matched, position-resolved on both sides and crosses the representation survival boundary**, and that is what recommends it despite six differing properties.

The sweep can also retire one confound and refute one candidate explanation without any new pair. If the two ProGen3 arms' per-block profiles resemble the ProGen2 and ProteinGLM survivors' rather than the non-survivors', the bfloat16 extraction confound is weakened at no cost. And if the ProGen2 ladder's five profiles place their increments at similar normalised depths across a 43× span in parameter count, that is an invariance no cross-checkpoint pair can supply.

## Which pairs survive a change in the provisional pattern

The 8-of-33 survival pattern is provisional and may change under recomputation, so each pair is stated with what happens to it if the pattern moves.

**Ranks 1, 2 and 5 survive.** Each rests on the likelihood side for at least one of its two outcome statements, and the gate reports the likelihood result as final: a likelihood enters a comparison as one scalar column, so the readout class barely bites on it, and no readout-class sweep or depth re-extraction can move the 14-of-33 count. Rank 5 is the strongest case. Its representation statement is a *null on both sides*, so recomputation can only leave it a likelihood pair, turn it into a representation pair as well if one side resolves, or invert it if the other does — and all three outcomes are informative. Rank 2's representation side is the fragile half: ProtGPT2 is resolved at two of three seeds, so it can lose that class without the pair losing its likelihood contrast.

**Rank 6 survives with a declared fallback.** Its likelihood contrast is final. Its representation contrast depends on ProGen2-xlarge remaining a survivor, and ProGen2-xlarge is the ProGen2 rung most likely to move: on the admitted readout endpoint its increment is the ladder's smallest, +0.00075 to +0.00647 against +0.00256 to +0.01341 for the other four rungs. Over C+P+`wall` the five rungs are reported only as a group range of +0.01006 to +0.01667, so which rung sits at the low end is not recorded. If ProGen2-xlarge moves, substituting ProGen2-large keeps the depth match at 32 blocks and loses the width match; substituting base or medium loses both.

**Ranks 3 and 4 do not survive.** Rank 4's outcome difference is a single seed and would be erased by any recomputation that moved RITA-XL's third seed either way. Rank 3 rests on ProGen3-112M, whose individual increment is not recorded — the gate reports the two ProGen3 arms only as a group range of +0.01046 to +0.02487 — and whose extraction is one of the panel's two bfloat16 cells under the singleton-only batching bound.

And if the reassessment widens the survivor set substantially, the 8-of-33 boundary dissolves rather than moves, the whole family of representation pairs loses its subject, and only the likelihood pairs — the ProLLaMA lineage and rank 5 — remain as targets. That is the strongest argument for running rank 5 now and holding ranks 3, 4 and 6 until the reassessment reports.

## Evidence and support

The property table and the separation report are produced by `scripts/transfer/assemble_matched_pair_properties.py`, in two subcommands so that the table is digest-bound before any outcome class is read; `separate` refuses a table whose bytes no longer hash to the declared digest. Both ran on the L20 host in the `ct` environment, CPU-only — 15.3 seconds of wall clock to build the table from the 323 MB admitted archive and 0.07 seconds to relate it — with no model load, no cohort draw, no GPU and no pod contention. Their outputs are `logs/matched_pair_handoff/arm_properties.json`, SHA-256 `da9009db8bff12b51f35577dd93e27a838ab250b3ba2c81e436d160ee477385b`, and `logs/matched_pair_handoff/separation.json`, SHA-256 `e3b3df9f78f32ec358bf5a907d53052652ff6f15466988be29aeb05324d8626c`. The inputs are the admitted expansion archive's 34 per-arm manifests and `src/transfer/arms.py`; the checkpoints themselves are not reachable from this host, which is why their configuration facts are transcribed from repository records or written `not_recorded`.

`tests/test_matched_pair_properties.py` covers the four conditions any number here depends on, plus the comparison rule's edges. Nineteen tests pass. Outcome-blindness is tested rather than asserted: emptying both outcome tables must leave the property table's digest unchanged. The digest binding is tested as a negative path: altering one property cell of a frozen table must make `separate` refuse by name. The transcription checks are tested as negative paths in both directions — a transcribed hidden width that disagrees with the `ArmSpec` declaration and an absent transcription must each stop the build rather than pick a side. The three-way comparison is tested on its edges: an unrecorded value on either side is undecidable rather than a difference, a property unrecorded on both sides of a boundary does not read as separating, and the parameter tolerance matches at 9.9% and differs at 10.7%. The remaining tests pin the roster size, the exclusion of the EC-conditioned arm, the disjointness of the outcome classes, the pair ranking's order and the canonical form of the digest.

The stage is declared in `NON_DRAWING_STAGES` in `tests/test_cohort_draw_contract.py`: it reads every arm of the roster and samples none, so rule 1's hazard is absent rather than answered.

## Limitations

The property set is bounded by what this host records. Attention structure is unrecorded for 8 of the 33 anchor arms, MLP structure for 2 and released storage precision for 28, so a pair's "undecidable" count is a statement about the repository and not about the checkpoints, and a pair could be better or worse matched than its row says on any of those axes. Head counts, normalisation form, positional scheme and per-layer intermediate width are not in the set at all, because no record states them for enough arms to compare. Parameter counts are nominal figures at different roundings compared within a 10% tolerance, and the stored-byte totals they are cross-checked against include container headers and any buffer serialised beside the weights — a GPT-2-lineage causal mask of 1024 by 1024 entries per block, for instance — so the ratio runs 1.993 to 4.195 stored bytes per nominal parameter across the panel and a checkpoint can read above the width its storage dtype implies.

`measured_segmentation` is a two-level class. The measured residues-per-token rate is recorded for only 9 of the 33 arms, so two arms can match on the class while differing materially in rate, as GPT-2-large and ProtGPT2 do.

The separation counts are census counts over an unmatched roster with correlated lineages, uncorrected across 17 properties, 33 arms, two endpoints and three split seeds. They do not estimate a model-population prevalence and they identify nothing. The outcome classes are transcribed from the gate's admitted 33-arm check at the resolution that document reports: the 8 all-seed survivors, the 2 two-of-three arms and the 9 unresolved arms are named there, while the 14 arms with at least one seed resolved below zero are reported as a count, so those 14 carry one class here and are not split further. Per-arm point estimates exist in the record only as group ranges for ProGen2 and ProGen3, which is why two of the six pairs cannot be assessed for fragility at the arm level.

Everything about the representation side is conditional on one fixed compressed linear readout class at two extraction depths — 1,024 fixed Gaussian projection coordinates of four pooled hidden-state summaries under linear ridge on the admitted penalty grid — at a label budget of 163 wild-type clusters, on one biological cohort whose grouping is single-linkage at 50% identity with 80% coverage and is neither certified family holdout nor free of pretraining overlap. No pair above, and no property that separates one, is evidence that any model's representations contain or lack a biological computation. A resolved increment is accessible predictive information beyond declared controls under a declared readout; it locates no mechanism, and identifying one is what the pairs are handed over for.
