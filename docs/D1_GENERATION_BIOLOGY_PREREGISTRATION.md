# Direction-1 generated-output biological evidence supplement

Date: 2026-09-05 (Asia/Shanghai). Campaigns: EXP-R2-232 and EXP-R2-233. This document specifies new endpoints before any generated-sequence structure predictions are inspected. The exact input manifests and this protocol's SHA-256 must be recorded before dispatch. A reservation or protocol is not a completed result.

## Question and scope

Do the declared native generation procedures produce sequences with measurable biological compatibility, and how strong is that behavioral evidence that the models have learned information useful for protein generation? The deliverable is a complete computational evidence package. Identifying particular learned biological rules or internal mechanisms belongs to Directions 2 and 3.

Profile membership, predicted structural plausibility and experimental expression/folding/function are different evidence levels. No new exact-sequence functional measurement is available in this campaign. ESMFold confidence is a predictor output, not observed folding or function. A bounded positive can support learned biological information; a negative bounds the model, decoding configuration, task and evaluator rather than proving no knowledge. Profiles themselves contain biological information: surpassing every profile or retrieval reference, and certifying complete training disjointness, are not universal knowledge-existence gates.

This is a supplement to R227, not a replacement. Its original classes, eligibility decisions, grouped rates and verdicts remain frozen. Reanalysis of already observed generation, profile and reference-search outputs is post-hoc descriptive analysis. Newly predicted structures have prospectively specified endpoints on a previously generated cohort; they are not independent new generation or independent experimental biology. The publication assessment follows the completed evidence rather than determining which outcomes are retained.

## Population, accounting and fixed sampling

**EXP-R2-232 full ledger.** Retain all 12,800 original protein requested/mismatched attempts (two models, 16 classes each, two conditions and 200 attempts per cell), the 400 original unconditioned floor attempts, and the available natural reference records. The primary class panel remains 14 ZymCTRL EC classes and 15 ProLLaMA superfamilies. The three originally ineligible classes remain visible but do not enter its primary aggregate. Empty, short, out-of-support and unrecognized outputs remain in their original attempt denominators. Existing missing per-output EOS metadata does not invalidate sequence-based endpoints; no EOS-defined biological-success claim is made.

Use the existing near-duplicate grouping for profile reporting and retain exact sequence hashes. Preserve class overlap and multi-label assignments; do not turn overlapping profiles into a single forced category. Report all eligible classes, including zero-yield classes. Different EC/superfamily ontologies are not differenced into a cross-model ranking. Floors use different native interfaces and are descriptive references, not same-model unconditioned causal controls.

**Predictor support.** Strict AA20, length 16–1024 residues inclusive, full input sequence, no cropping or sequence repair. Outputs outside support are `not_evaluable`, not biologically failed. Long natural proteins are never cropped into apparent whole-protein positive controls. Natural reference lengths may not overlap generated lengths in a class; report that coverage gap and do not treat an unmatched natural–generated contrast as a model effect.

**Control-only pilot.** Select at most two natural records per eligible arm/class and one fixed composition shuffle for each, at most 116 predictions. Use only these controls for import/numerical verification, support/timing inspection and evaluator attainability. No generated structure endpoint is inspected during calibration. Main natural controls must be exact-sequence-disjoint from the pilot globally, including shared references across classes. Shortfalls are recorded, not filled by choosing favorable controls.

**Main structure sample.** In every eligible arm/class/condition, randomly sample 16 supported generated outputs, or all supported outputs if fewer. Stratify by lengths 16–128, 129–256, 257–512 and 513–1024. Allocate by fixed largest remainders with at least one sampled record per nonempty stratum, using a frozen seed and deterministic tie-breaks. Selection uses no profile hit, reference distance, generation likelihood or predicted score. Record population size, sample size and inclusion probability within each stratum. Add one composition-shuffled sequence for each sampled generation and eight supported natural references per eligible arm/class plus their paired shuffles. This is at most 2,320 main predictions before exact-hash cache reuse. Do not add samples because their outcomes are unfavorable or uncertain.

Composition shuffles preserve full length and amino-acid counts. They are a simple sequence-order reference, not experimentally certified nonfolders. Degenerate unchanged shuffles remain explicit and contribute a zero paired contrast where predictions agree; do not repeatedly shuffle until the predictor dislikes a sequence. Natural references are biological sequence examples, not evidence that every reference has the desired functional measurement.

## Predictor and frozen endpoints

Use the staged [`facebook/esmfold_v1`](https://huggingface.co/facebook/esmfold_v1) checkpoint at revision `75a3841ee059df2bf4d56688166c8fb459ddd97a`, weight SHA-256 `2ee07356b125d1e3e57503c204111fd7323347fc4735d41d3caac57c2a78e116`, through the validated Transformers 4.57.3 implementation. Freeze ESM stem float16, folding trunk float32, no autocast, TF32 disabled, chunk size 128, singleton inference and four trunk passes (`num_recycles=3`). Singleton inference avoids padding-dependent pTM aggregation. Store per-residue CA pLDDT on the 0–100 scale, the CA mean, fraction of residues with CA pLDDT at least 70, pTM, full PAE, coordinates, masks, status and input identity. [The pinned Transformers implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/v4.57.3/src/transformers/models/esm/modeling_esmfold.py) computes categorical lDDT on 0–1 internally; the runner converts explicitly, rather than guessing scale from observed values. Arithmetic/interface repairs require a documented control-only check before resuming.

**Primary new structural estimand, separately per R227 model:** the equal-class mean of the length-stratum-weighted paired difference in mean CA pLDDT between requested generated outputs and their own composition shuffles. The weights recover each cell's supported generated-output distribution. This comparison holds length and composition exactly within pairs. Its interpretation is a predictor-confidence difference beyond that implemented sequence-order reference, not a structure/function observation or identification of the learned rule.

Secondary continuous quantities are the fraction of residues above CA pLDDT 70, pTM, and the analogous paired differences for mismatched generation and natural controls. Report requested and mismatched structural behavior separately; both conditions can generate structurally plausible proteins, so a positive requested-minus-mismatched structural difference is not a required success condition. Existing requested-minus-mismatched profile effects remain the evidence for native label control.

For descriptive yields, define the **predicted-confidence event** as mean CA pLDDT at least 70 and at least 80% of residues individually at or above 70. This fixed operational event is not a validated fold/function threshold, and pTM does not supply another hard gate. Show its performance on pilot and main natural/shuffled controls. Report the continuous distributions beside the event so an arbitrary cutoff cannot carry the finding alone. Do not tune this threshold on generation outcomes.

The complete profile ledger reports per-class all-attempt target recognition, any-profile recognition, full class confusion including multi-label and no-hit states, exact/near-duplicate group counts, and number of distinct target-recognized groups per 200 attempts. Structure reporting includes support, prediction success/failure, continuous distributions, confidence-event rates and observed distinct qualifying groups. Do not extrapolate a count of all structurally qualified unique clusters from a 16-sequence sample; observed qualifying clusters are a lower bound on the observed full batch, not an unbiased census.

The profile-target AND predicted-confidence joint event is descriptive, never called functional yield. Use known full-ledger target-hit counts within length strata to bound/calibrate the sampled joint estimate; strata with no sampled target hits despite population target hits remain unknown. Do not report a sampled joint rate above the known full-population target rate. Both profile-only and structural endpoints remain visible.

## Calibration, uncertainty and missingness

The pilot asks whether this implementation returns valid full-length structures and distinguishes natural sequences from their own shuffles in its declared support. Report per-arm/class support and paired effects; require a positive lower 95% class-bootstrap bound for the arm's mean natural-minus-shuffle CA-pLDDT difference before using the arm's structure comparison as positive biological evidence. This is an evaluator-attainability condition, not a model gate. An arm with fewer than eight evaluable pilot classes or an inconclusive contrast is reported as uncalibrated; do not inspect generated predictions to rescue calibration or pick a new threshold. The main natural panel provides separately held-out calibration results, not an independent generation replicate.

Point estimates use the frozen inclusion probabilities and preserve the full attempt denominator. For sampled continuous structure endpoints, also report their supported-input denominator and the number of complete pairs. A technical prediction failure is not a biological negative. Missing prediction rows prevent a report from being marked complete; explicit terminal errors or unsupported inputs are counted with reasons. Primary arm-level structural interpretation requires complete paired predictions in its frozen supported sample; otherwise provide the observed subset and bounded missing-outcome sensitivity without a confirmatory label.

Use 4,000 deterministic class-cluster bootstrap resamples, seed 20260905. Resample classes with replacement, keeping each class's requested/mismatched/reference data together; this prevents hundreds of residues or duplicate sequences from being treated as independent biological replicates. Report per-class estimates and the class distribution, not only a pooled mean. Intervals describe variation across the declared class cohort and do not establish random sampling of all protein families or uncertainty over retraining the model. For the two primary model contrasts, show both 95% descriptive intervals and 97.5% intervals for the family of two prespecified contrasts. If interpreting statistical sign, use the latter. No significance gate determines whether the full negative or mixed result is retained.

Retain all sequence-, pair- and class-level sufficient statistics required to reconstruct the estimates and intervals. Provide an additional near-duplicate-balanced descriptive comparison on the selected sequence groups, labelled as a changed weighting rather than the same production-yield estimand. The class bootstrap does not create an independent decoding seed or remove shared-training/evaluator dependencies.

For event rates, report weighted estimates within support and all-attempt support/missing-outcome bounds. These incorporate the sampled rate estimate and are not exact census bounds for unpredicted sequences. In unsupported or technically missing inputs, do not silently assign a structural failure or impute the mean of the successful subset. Profile recognition is observed on the full ledger and needs no structural imputation.

## Reference distance and generalization

Keep the exact searched corpus/version and distinguish no alignment, aligned identity, missing search and missing alignment coverage. A no-hit record is not an observed zero-percent identity alignment. Analyze identity bands only within declared search support, with query/target coverage where retained or newly measured. Show confidence/recognition distributions across reference distance without selecting a low-distance survivor set as the main denominator. Very high similarity limits claims of transfer; low similarity to this reference does not certify absence from all training data.

Natural–generated comparisons require visible length/class support. Paired self-shuffle contrasts remain interpretable when natural length matching is unavailable, but calibration cannot be borrowed from an unrepresented sequence regime without a limitation. No novel biological law, catalytic-site probe, memorization mechanism or full-training-disjoint claim is part of this campaign.

## EXP-R2-233: one newer native generation point

The reserved branch uses ProGen3-3B through one verified author-native task, with complete raw attempts and stopping reasons. No EC/IPR tag is imposed on an interface that does not support it. The native task and decoding settings are a separate pre-output amendment after interface qualification; until then this branch is not frozen for execution. The planned finite scale is 800 attempts and a score-independent 128-sequence structural sample with paired shuffles under the same predictor support. Its unconditioned or sequence-conditioned task must not be ranked against the R227 EC/IPR tasks as if they shared labels. The branch is independently generated but not a new biological experimental validation.
