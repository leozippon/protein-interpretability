# A reusable qualification harness for order-k interaction endpoints

The higher-order gate has closed twice as **unresolved rather than not-detected**, and both closures came from qualifying the endpoint rather than from measuring a model. The [MegaScale pilot](D1_GATE_HIGHER_ORDER.md) found zero substitution variants of order three or above in `dataset2` — every apparent one an indel construct whose `aa_seq` is truncated to wild-type length — and was left with two independent site pairs on a cross-background route. The [ProteinGym extension](D1_GATE_HIGHER_ORDER_EXTENDED.md) found 206,390 complete cubes and still stopped: its best-qualified support reached a shared-to-discordance ratio of 0.276 [0.000, 0.496] at 119.6 Kish effective site triples on 598 site triples, against the admitted pairwise endpoint's 2.78 [2.34, 3.26] at a comparable 121.6, while on that same assay the order-2 shared component **is** resolved above zero at 0.0596 [0.0232, 0.0800] scaled-fitness units. The third-order failure there is an order property of a well-powered assay's labels, not an assay property.

Both closures asked the same questions and answered them with code written twice. This document owns the harness that asks them once, of any candidate, and the assessment of the one candidate not previously reached. **Its verdict on that candidate is stop at every declared order**, and at order two the reason is not the noise floor but the unit a model comparison generalises over. It measures no model quantity: no likelihood, no representation and no score enters anything here, and no GPU was scheduled. The two gate records own their own findings; the [protocol](D1_INFORMATION_HIERARCHY_PROTOCOL.md) owns the estimand discipline and the [label instrument](D1_PAIRWISE_LABEL_INSTRUMENT.md) owns the between-channel decomposition this reuses.

## What the harness asks, and where it refuses

The five questions run in order, and the answer to each is a measured quantity rather than a judgement.

**Does the candidate carry complete lower-order support, and how much.** Completeness is the requirement that every non-empty proper subset of a variant's substitutions is itself a measured state, checked level by level from the singles upwards and stopped at the first incomplete level. The harness reports the whole distribution of that depth per order, not only the complete count, because "no cube exists" and "cubes are one level short" are different findings about a library's design. The contrast and the completeness rule are imported from `src/transfer/higher_order_cycle.py` and `src/transfer/proteingym_higher_order.py`; neither is reimplemented.

**How many independent site tuples, across how many positions.** Never cube counts. Every cube on one site tuple reuses that tuple's whole lower-order series, so cubes on one tuple are not independent draws, and 25,035 complete triples once rested on the C(4, 3) = 4 site triples a four-position library has. The harness prints that combinatorial ceiling beside every tuple count, and reports the positions and the distinct substitutions the cubes are built from as well, because the tuples of one library themselves reuse the same singles.

**Does it carry a genuine replicate channel or only counts.** Four declared kinds, with the distinction stated rather than described. A `replicate` channel — separate cultures, sorts, growth replicates, synonymous genotypes — supports a measured floor. A `dispersion` column supports a propagated floor and no between-channel decomposition. A `count` channel supports a multinomial model of sequencing and library sampling, which says nothing about assay-level systematic error, batch structure or the selection's own nonlinearity: that floor is a **lower bound on total measurement noise**, and the harness never grants clearance against it, because the Wu 2016 support cleared its counting-noise floor by a factor of 6.49 at order three and was still not qualified. `none` supports nothing.

**What is the order-k noise floor.** Measured and propagated, both reported. The measured floor forms the cycle independently in each channel of a declared pair and decomposes it by the admitted between-channel construction, grouped on the site tuple. The propagated floor is `sqrt(2 ** k) * sigma` from the pooled within-state per-channel spread over exactly the corner states that order's cubes use: an order-k cycle sums `2 ** k` corner estimates where a pairwise cycle sums four, so the floor grows with order and cannot be inherited from a pairwise measurement. The propagation assumes independent corner errors, which is false wherever cubes share corners, so the ratio of measured to propagated is reported as the statement it is — a measurement of how correlated the corner errors are. On ProteinGym it ran 0.76 to 0.82; on this candidate it runs 0.42 to 0.61, where the reuse is heavier.

**Does the endpoint carry the unit a model comparison needs.** Two units govern an order-k endpoint and conflating them is how a noise-limited support becomes a scheduled run. The **site tuple** is the unit the label instrument's noise floor lives at, because every cube on one tuple reuses that tuple's lower-order series. The **wild-type family group** is the unit a model comparison generalises over, because a fit trained on one member of a family and tested on another measures family membership rather than the quantity. A support can be rich in the first and empty in the second: 858 site pairs inside one domain is one family group, the frozen five-outer, four-inner held-group fold design has no second group to hold out, and a family bootstrap has one unit to draw. The harness counts both, floors both at eight, and reports them separately so neither can be read as the other. References of equal length are compared position by position; references of different lengths are counted as distinct, which makes the group count an upper bound — an alignment could only merge further, so a refusal that fires on it is sound and a pass on it is not, and the record says which it is.

**Does the endpoint clear that floor.** Clearance is the conservative reading: the shared-to-discordance ratio's lower interval endpoint must resolve above one against *one channel's* noise. One is the boundary rather than a tuned figure, because the shared component is an upper bound on reproducible interaction signal and the discordance is a lower bound on per-channel noise, so a ratio of an upper bound over a lower bound below one supports no statement at all. The generous reading against the **pooled label's** noise is computed and reported beside it — the pooled label over n channels carries one channel's noise divided by `sqrt(n)`, and the ratio scales linearly in that denominator, so the point estimate and both endpoints multiply through exactly — and it never grants clearance on its own, because a model comparison fitted to a pooled label still has to be read against the reproducibility of what it was fitted to.

**Clearing the floor is necessary and not sufficient.** Scheduling requires both floors, so an order can clear its noise floor and still schedule nothing; the record then says which of the two failed, and the verdict carries the orders in that state under their own name rather than letting them read as clearances.

**Stop is the default, and it is enforced per order.** `candidate_verdict` reports a clearance per declared order and `assert_scheduling_admitted` requires the order it is asked about. A candidate whose order-2 endpoint clears and whose order-3 endpoint does not is exactly the state both closed gates were in, and a caller that read one boolean off the whole candidate would schedule the run the floor refuses. Four further conditions refuse rather than warn: a support below the package's eight-unit resampling floor withholds its interval and therefore cannot clear; a block whose reference sequence is not itself a measured state is refused, because corner `()` of every cube would be unmeasured and only the centred-residual estimand would be available; a state without a finite value in every declared channel is excluded and counted; and two clearing cells drawn from one duplicated library are refused, because two tables of one library are not two units of evidence.

Two stages keep the support outcome-blind. `declare` reads state keys only — replacing every value with a constant leaves its output byte-identical, which is asserted rather than described — and writes a declaration whose SHA-256 the qualification stage refuses to proceed without, re-deriving the same cube count at every order first. The BLAS thread count is pinned at four, the count the admitted projection ran at, and the realised thread pools are recorded per artefact.

## The candidate: Escobedo, Voigt, Faure and Lehner 2025

Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.16266645`, CC-BY-4.0, two archives of 19,726,227 bytes (md5 `6f0626ae43afba3b03087593197dc3a3`) and 2,226,162,297 bytes (md5 `e7d6433bf7cca53071836a097ca63069`, sha256 `c6692a0b1b2eca1e53b93f6d4d92299a766ac0c46940ab53a149507fa8571283`). Both were already staged by `scripts/transfer/acquire_endpoint_datasets.py` at their pinned checksums; the harness reads members out of the archive, so none of the 6.689 GB of its 3,682 members was unpacked. Storage was unconstrained and the shared volume stood at 341 GiB free on 97% use throughout.

Seven per-replicate DiMSum tables were declared, covering the randomized-core, randomized-surface, sparse designed-core, suppressor and single-protein libraries for the abundance endpoint. They split into 20 measurement blocks by sequence length, of which 13 carry a measured reference and 7 do not. The channel is `replicate`: two or three `fitness<i>_uncorr` columns, separately grown and separately sequenced repeats of one amino-acid state. State keys are derived from the sequences against the block's reference and never from the deposition's own order columns, which are not reliable here — one table marks all 9,973 of its equal-length rows `indel`, the mirror image of MegaScale's truncated `aa_seq`.

### The randomized-core libraries carry no lower-order support at all

This is the decisive finding, and it is a different mechanism from MegaScale's zero rather than the same one. The libraries the deposition is named for randomise every core or surface position at once, so their variants are concentrated at orders 8 to 11 (core, 15 varying positions) and 16 to 20 (surface, 21 positions) — and the singles and doubles those cubes would need were never measured.

| `core_randomization/len57` | 13,527 | 15 | 1 | 0 | 0 for all 16 |
| `surface_randomization/len57` | — | 21 | refused | refused | reference unmeasured |
| `buried_and_exposed_randomization/len57` | 57,793 | 52 | 1 | 0 | 0 for all 17 |

A support depth of 0 means at least one constituent single substitution is absent, so the failure is not "one level short" but "no ladder at all". The surface block is refused outright: its modal sequence is not a measured state, so corner `()` of every cube is unmeasured. The randomized-core design that makes these libraries attractive — every buried position varied simultaneously — is exactly what removes their lower-order support.

Requiring every channel, at every one of `2 ** k` corners, conditions the support on resolvability in all of them, and that selection runs against exactly the strongly interacting combinations.

**Order 2 clears the noise floor on two blocks, and those two blocks are one protein.** The suppressor block reaches 1.527 [1.328, 1.728] on 858 site pairs at 396.3 Kish effective units, and the sparse designed-core block 1.688 [1.326, 1.967] on the whole of C(7, 2). They are nonetheless **one wild-type family group**: their references are the same FYN SH3 domain at 51 of 57 identical positions, 89.5% identity, far above the grouping contract's 50%. **Order 3 does not clear, and it is the closest any candidate has come.** The primary reading is 1.034 [0.938, 1.135] on the best-powered support in the programme — a point estimate above one whose lower endpoint is not. The generous pooled-label reading is 1.792 [1.625, 1.966], so under that reading the endpoint would clear; the conservative reading is the one the verdict rests on, and the gap between them is 1.73. Orders 4 and 5 then fall monotonically to 0.743 and 0.574 as the noise grows with `sqrt(2 ** k)` while the reproducible component stays near 0.07.

That is precisely why the primary pair is declared from the channel names before any value is read: taking the clearing pair would be an outcome-selected reading. The honest statement is that the order-3 endpoint on this candidate is **borderline and channel-pair dependent**, between 0.947 and 1.704 depending on which two of three replicates are compared.

## Expand or stop, with the cost

**Stop, at every declared order. No model scoring is scheduled on this candidate.**

At order three the conservative reading does not clear on the best-powered support this programme has measured, at eight times the pairwise endpoint's effective site-tuple count, and the reading is unstable across the three available channel pairs. Orders four and five are clearly below their floors. An energy scale would not change this: the deposition's fitted free energies are inferred from these same fitness measurements by one global MoCHI fit, so they supply no independent per-state channel — a cycle computed on them would report the fit's own structure and its uncertainty would be a fit uncertainty. That is the concrete form of the general point: an energy scale fixes the **estimand**, not the signal-to-noise ratio.

At order two the noise floor is cleared and the endpoint still schedules nothing, and the reason is worth stating carefully because it is the kind of comparison this harness exists to prevent one level up. It is tempting to read the suppressor block's 858 site pairs at Kish 396.3 against the admitted pairwise endpoint's 121.6 as more than three times the power. The two numbers are not the same quantity. The admitted 121.6 is a Kish effective site-pair count *across 64 wild-type family groups* under equal weight per group; the 396.3 is a Kish effective site-pair count *inside one domain*. On the unit a model comparison generalises over, this support has **one** group where the admitted endpoint has 64, so the frozen held-group fold design cannot be instantiated at all and a family bootstrap has one unit to draw. A site-pair count inside one protein is not a substitute for a family count, exactly as a cube count is not a substitute for a site-tuple count.

The cross-family version does not rescue it either, and this is measured rather than assumed. At most seven distinct backgrounds in this deposition carry a substantive order-2 support — below the eight-unit floor before anything else is asked — and only the one FYN SH3 family among them clears the noise floor; the other six sit between 0.000 and 1.491 with intervals reaching below one. Pooling them at equal weight per group would be dominated by the six that do not clear.

The cost is recorded so it is not rediscovered. The corner states of the suppressor block's order-2 support are 2,530 distinct sequences of 57 residues, and of its order-3 support 4,184; scaling the recorded pairwise-panel budget by sequence count puts an order-2 cell at roughly 0.9 to 1.0 H200-hours of forwards across 33 arms at batch size one and an order-3 cell at 1.5 to 1.6, plus the same CPU fitting budget. Those are proportional estimates from a recorded budget on sequences of 37 to 72 residues, not measurements. **That cost was not spent.** What would make this candidate's order-2 endpoint spendable is not compute but more families: eight or more wild-type groups below 50% identity, each carrying a complete order-2 support that clears its own floor. This deposition supplies one.

## What would qualify an order-k endpoint, and whether the record supplies it

The three closed assessments, taken together, specify what a qualifying deposition would look like: **eight or more wild-type families below 50% identity, each carrying substantive complete order-2 or higher support, with a genuine replicate or dispersion channel reaching every corner of the contrast.** Breadth, completeness and a corner-covering channel, together — and it is the conjunction that is hard, because each is easy alone.

Five depositions have now been measured against it. Three were assessed by the earlier gates and two by the survey stage below, which reads state keys and repeat structure only and never a measured value.

| Deposition | Families with complete order-2+ support | Highest complete order | Channel | Fails on |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| MegaScale `dataset2` | 64 | 2 | two proteases, genuine | **order**: zero substitution variants of order ≥3 exist |
| MGnify Stability (Cho 2026) | 0 of thousands of domains | none closes | two proteases with 95% intervals on every state | **completeness**: 104,360 designed doubles, no cycle closes |
| ProteinGym, 217 assays | 9 assays carry order-3 cubes | 9 | 3 of the 9 carry a replicate or dispersion channel | **channel coverage**, then the floor on both measurable assays |
| Escobedo 2025 | 1 clearing, ≤7 present | 5 | 2–3 growth replicates, genuine | **breadth**: one family group at 89.5% identity |
| SKEMPI v2 | 78 at order 2, 10 at order 3 | 3 | independent publications re-measuring a state | **corner coverage**: 6 order-2 contrasts in 4 complexes, 0 at order 3 |

Two of these are new measurements and both are informative beyond a refusal.

**MGnify Stability has the best channel in the record and no closed cycle at all.** Its 2,262,422 measured states each carry a trypsin and a chymotrypsin free energy with a 95% interval on each — the admitted label instrument's own channel structure, on two orders of magnitude more states than MegaScale — across three libraries and domain lengths 43 to 80. It declares 104,360 rows as double-mutant designs. **Zero complete order-2 cycles close.** The reason is measured rather than inferred, because a zero is only a finding once it is separated from a defect: over 400 sampled designed doubles, every one has **zero** measured single-substitution neighbours, while over 400 sampled states drawn from the deposition at large, 250 have at least one and some have up to 12. Single-mutant series are abundant; the designed doubles sit apart from them, so the corners a cycle needs were never measured beside the double.

**SKEMPI v2 has breadth and completeness and a channel that does not reach the corners.** Across 348 complexes and 7,085 published entries it carries 4,343 single, 861 double and 309 triple mutant states, and complete lower-order support for **610 order-2 contrasts in 78 complexes** — 19 of which carry eight or more — and **45 order-3 contrasts in 10 complexes**. The wild-type corner is measured by construction, since every entry reports the unmutated complex's affinity beside the mutant's. Its channel is a state re-measured by two or more independent publications, which is independence of laboratory and method rather than of protease, and 374 of 6,533 states have it. But a contrast needs it at *every* corner, and that holds for **6 order-2 contrasts across 4 complexes and none at all at order 3**. No complex reaches eight channelled contrasts at either order.

**The structural reason is a three-way tension, and it is visible in the table rather than asserted.** A library that spans many families keeps its per-family budget small and therefore stays at order one or two — and MGnify shows the extreme case, where a designed-double partition was added without the singles that would close its cycles. A library that reaches high order does so by randomising a few positions of one protein simultaneously, which is exactly what leaves the lower orders unmeasured, and when a designed sparse set does keep them it keeps them in one family. A curated cross-family compilation inherits breadth and completeness from the literature but its repeat structure is incidental to the aggregation and does not follow the contrasts. Each design purchases at most two of the three properties the endpoint needs. A bounded check of the combinatorial-mutagenesis literature is consistent: comprehensive second-order libraries are built one domain at a time, and the breadth-first scans that span hundreds of domains are single-substitution scans.

**The finding, stated as it should enter the capability map.** Higher-order interaction is **measurement-limited, with the limit characterised**: the endpoint is exactly constructible, its noise floor is measurable wherever a channel exists, and on every deposition assessed it fails qualification before any model quantity is read — each for a different and quantified reason. That is a stronger and more useful claim than a weak positive would have been, because it says where the limit is and what would lift it. It is not a statement about any model, and it is not a null: no model quantity has been read against an order-k endpoint at all.

## Limitations

- The order-3 verdict rests on a declared choice among three channel pairs whose readings span 0.947 to 1.704. The choice is outcome-blind and the spread is reported, but a candidate whose channels disagree this much is qualified less firmly than the interval alone suggests.
- The three replicate channels share library, growth protocol, sequencing run and the deposition's own normalisation. Error common to all three inflates the shared component and is invisible to the channel differences, so every ratio here is an upper bound over a lower bound and nothing establishes accuracy against an external thermodynamic measurement.
- The scale is a DiMSum growth-rate-derived fitness, not an energy. Nonadditivity on it is not by itself a molecular interaction, the floor is measured on the same scale so the comparison is internally consistent, and global assay nonlinearity is not removed.
- Six of the thirteen admitted blocks are keyed against a modal library background rather than a wild type, so their contrast is an interaction among substitutions away from a designed core and their fitness scale is uncertified.
- Requiring a finite value in every channel at every corner excludes 47,951 rows across the deposition and conditions the support against the strongly interacting combinations, more strongly at order k than at order 2 in proportion to `2 ** k`.
- Site tuples are the resampling unit, and the tuples of one block still reuse its 101 to 181 underlying substitutions. The Kish counts are effective tuple counts under that weighting and not effective counts of independent molecules.
- The suppressor result is a one-block result in one protein, and the 1,554 site triples are subsets of 49 positions rather than 1,554 independent position samples.
- No model was scored, so nothing here states anything about any checkpoint's likelihood assignments or representations. No fold-identity or held-out-label leakage test on a fitted readout exists, because no readout was fitted; that is recorded as absent rather than satisfied. The leakage-adjacent condition that does exist is tested: the admitted support is a function of the measured state keys and never of their values.
- The family grouping here is computed from the library references position by position, which is exact for equal-length references and unavailable for unequal ones; unequal-length references are counted as distinct, so the group count is an upper bound and the refusals rest on that bound rather than on a run of the programme's own single-linkage grouping contract over an alignment.
- The survey's family counts are computed from library references position by position within equal lengths and treat unequal lengths as distinct, so they are upper bounds on merging rather than a run of the programme's grouping contract over an alignment. SKEMPI's complexes are counted as distinct families by their PDB identity without a sequence-identity check between them, which for a compilation containing several antibody-antigen and protease-inhibitor complexes is an upper bound too.
- SKEMPI's channel is independence of laboratory and method, which is stronger than two proteases in one respect and confounded by temperature and assay method in another; neither confound was quantified, because the channel does not reach enough corners for the question to arise.
- The Fig. 7 binding tables are a second trait on overlapping libraries and were not assessed; the harness was pointed at the abundance endpoint, which is the one comparable with MegaScale stability and ProteinGym fitness.

## Artefacts, tests and entry points

`src/transfer/higher_order_qualification.py` holds the harness; `scripts/transfer/qualify_higher_order_candidate.py` runs its two stages; `tests/test_higher_order_qualification.py` holds the conditions that must always hold. Twenty-three tests pass. They fix the unit as the site tuple against a four-position library that carries more cubes than tuples, fix the combinatorial ceiling, assert that the support inventory is byte-identical when every value is replaced by a constant, refuse an undeclared channel kind, refuse clearance on a count-derived floor and on a `dispersion` or absent channel, check that the propagated floor grows as `sqrt(2 ** k)` and that it is met on planted independent noise, check that the pooled-label reading scales the point and both endpoints by `sqrt(n)`, check that the primary channel pair is fixed by the channel names, and check the adapter's refusals — an unequal length, a non-canonical residue, a duplicate state, a channel gap, and a block whose reference is not measured. The negative paths that carry the verdict are exercised directly: a support below the eight-unit floor withholds its interval and cannot clear; a noise-limited endpoint names its measurement limitation and `assert_scheduling_admitted` raises; an order-2 clearance raises when asked to license order 3, and an unqualified order raises when asked at all; two clearing cells from one duplicated library raise rather than counting twice; and two blocks that clear the noise floor in one wild-type family schedule nothing, while eight distinct-reference families that clear it do, so the new refusal is exercised in both directions.
