# The Direction-1 to Direction-2 mechanistic interface

Direction 1 closed with two outcomes on one 33-arm panel that do not line up. The **likelihood** boundary is perfectly separated by protein pretraining, 14 of 14 inside and 0 of 19 outside, and the gate reports that result as final. The **representation** boundary is separated by nothing but architecture family, which is a lineage label and therefore circular, and the ProGen2 ladder — five rungs surviving across 12 to 32 blocks, 1,024 to 4,096 width and 151M to 6.44B parameters — refutes any scale, depth, width or precision account of it. The [matched-pair handoff](D1_MATCHED_PAIR_HANDOFF.md) records that **no well-matched pair exists for the representation boundary** and that transplant feasibility fails for all six candidate pairs as a matter of record, which is why transplant work stays on the [ProLLaMA lineage](D2_PROLLAMA_CAPABILITY_LOCALIZATION.md) with Galactica-1.3B against InstructProtein as the one non-Llama likelihood replication.

This document owns the interface, not an experiment. Nothing here transplants, patches, fits or scores: it is the set of conditions a mechanism cell has to pass so that its result composes with the capability result rather than sitting beside it, and the one measurement that decides whether the recommended pair can be transplanted at all. The handoff owns the pairs and their ranking; the [depth sweep](D1_READOUT_DEPTH_SWEEP.md) owns the per-block design this composes with; the [readout report](D1_READOUT_RESULTS.md) owns the panel. Nothing here restates an increment those documents carry.

**Representation-level verdicts are deferred.** The programme's readout reassessment is in flight, and its first depth results are reported — to this work, ahead of the depth-sweep document's own Results section, which is still pending — to place three arms above zero at blocks the admitted extraction never hooked. The 8-of-33 survival pattern the pairs rest on may therefore change, and the last section states which pairs are robust to that and which are not.

## One endpoint, so that a mechanism result composes

Every cell reports the same quantity, and the interface assembles it from the modules that declare it rather than restating it: the split seeds, fold counts, ridge grid, projection contract and bootstrap come from `src/transfer/pairwise_epistasis.py` and `src/transfer/readout_class_sweep.py`, and the two modules that both declare the split seeds are required to agree, which is the transcription check the handoff's property assembler uses for the same reason — a second copy of a constant is a second source of truth until something compares them.

The endpoint is the within-assay Spearman of the fitted predictor, or of the native mutant-minus-wild-type summed target log likelihood for a likelihood cell, against the measured effect; averaged within wild-type family at 50% identity and then equally over families. The support is the frozen 201-assay, 163-cluster, 25,728-variant anchor with cohort SHA-256 `4093ac34368cd9e7be02e5c84a22655ce78cb9bb4e4d58d626f281868dbe7992`, which was re-verified against the cohort on disk. Five deterministic outer and four inner wild-type-cluster folds at split seeds 20260923, 20260924 and 20260925, the inner seed being the outer seed plus 100 plus the zero-based outer-fold index. Within-assay standardized average-rank targets; equal weight per wild-type cluster, per assay within a cluster and per variant within an assay; training-only weighted feature scaling; the admitted five-point ridge grid with ties broken toward the stronger penalty; the fixed representation-projection seeds 20260923 through 20260926 at 256 coordinates per block under a pinned BLAS thread count of four; and 2,000 paired bootstrap draws at seed 20260923 over the unit **wild-type family at 50% identity**. The binding control is C+P+`wall` at +0.47955, +0.47906 and +0.48290 over the three seeds.

Two properties of that contract are not conveniences. The BLAS thread count is part of it because a float32 matrix product's reduction order depends on it and the admitted projection ran at four threads; the depth sweep measured a whole-panel product departing from the per-assay one by 3.483e-08, above the 1e-8 tolerance the pipeline-identity control uses, so the reduction order belongs to the contract rather than to the host. And every row-identity digest renders its target through `float` before `repr`, because `repr(np.float64(0.5))` is `0.5` under NumPy 1.26 and `np.float64(0.5)` under NumPy 2: a NumPy-scalar rendering hashes identical rows to different digests across interpreters and makes the check unreliable in both directions. That matters concretely here — the compatibility census runs in a pod on NumPy 2 while the fitting runs on NumPy 1.26.

**A support mismatch is already visible and is a refusal rather than a footnote.** The ProLLaMA lineage's admitted endpoints are on its own 211-assay, 169-family native support, not on the anchor: the parent at −0.00740 [−0.02713, +0.01226] and Stage 1 at +0.14604 [+0.11814, +0.17435]. A cell scored on 211 assays does not compose with an anchor capability result, and `check_support_identity` says so. The remedy is cheap and needs no new forward pass, because each transplanted cell already retains its per-variant likelihood delta per assay: restrict the existing cell records to the anchor's assays and recompute the endpoint there. Whether that restriction is available depends on the anchor's 201 assays being contained in the native 211, which this repository does not record — the handoff carries the anchor's *cohort* digest and the transplant design carries the native support's *assay-id* digest `81432626b0fb4e711fccea81c0aa786c7d1d69b2dee7f935e2f3aee6ffb282fe`, and no artefact on this host carries the anchor's assay-id digest. A caller supplies it from the admitted per-arm manifest; that gap is recorded rather than worked around, and its consequence is worth stating plainly rather than leaving to inference: **a support mismatch is caught and a wrong-but-self-consistent digest is not.** If a caller computes the digest over the very assay list it then scores, the check passes by construction and certifies only that the cell scored what it said it scored — not that what it scored is the anchor. The check binds a cell to the anchor only when the digest reaching it comes from the admitted manifest rather than from the cell's own support, and nothing in the interface can tell those two provenances apart. Recording the anchor's assay-id digest in a repository artefact, once, is what would turn this from a convention into a refusal.

## The refusals

Each is a function that raises with a named reason, and `assert_cell_admitted` runs them together so a caller cannot publish a recovered fraction with only some of them checked.

**Floor.** The empty selection leaves the recipient untouched, and every live tensor digested off the loaded model must carry the recipient's census digest — exact equality per tensor, by name, with missing and unexpected names refused as well as mismatched ones. Exactness rather than a tolerance, because both ends are copies of stored values and the forward is deterministic at batch size one. The floor must then reproduce the recipient's admitted endpoint inside its admitted interval.

**Ceiling.** The full selection reconstructs the donor bit-identically under the same per-tensor check, and reproduces the donor's admitted endpoint inside its interval. A recovered fraction read against a scale that does not reconstruct the admitted models means nothing, so the analysis computes none if either end falls outside.

**Tensor-level provenance for every transplanted group, with its parameter count.** Each group names its tensors, its parameter count summed from the census's own element counts, its differing-element count and the source digest each tensor must carry. Three refusals live here: a group whose tensors are byte-identical across the two checkpoints is an algebraic no-op and must not be run, because a zero contrast from an identity is not evidence that the group is insensitive; a group whose shapes differ is refused rather than resized; and an undeclared group name is refused. The `complement:` spelling that makes a necessity arm and a sufficiency arm one code path stays in `capability_transplant.resolve_groups`, and the interface takes an already-resolved selection rather than duplicating the parser. That resolver's group universe is the Llama partition, and it is **left that way as a declared boundary** rather than extended, because the census has now established that no non-Llama transplant is licensed at all: rank 5 is refused on three grounds below, ranks 3, 4 and 6 span architecture families so no tensor correspondence exists, and rank 2's two vocabularies share a size but no content, so a cell crossing its embedding mixes two token-id semantics and any cell that does not is a parameter-space contrast rather than a capability transfer. A non-Llama complement cell has nothing to be the complement of. If a non-Llama transplant ever becomes licensed, extending that one resolver is the prerequisite.

**Support and fold identity against the admitted fits.** The support digest over the scored assay identifiers and the count must both match; the outer fold map's held-group membership must hash to the admitted fold identity under one declared spelling — each fold's members sorted as strings, fold order preserved, canonical JSON — and the fold count must match. The admitted fits digest the same content under their own group vocabulary, `held_groups` in the pairwise and global-context fits and `held_families` in the retrieval-strata analysis, so the interface takes the membership lists and the digest is comparable by construction rather than by two spellings agreeing.

**No held-out label reaches any tuning step.** The admitted nested fit partitions the *training* groups for penalty selection, so an outer fold's held groups appear in none of its inner folds. That is checked on membership rather than trusted from the construction, because a refit that rebuilt the inner partition over all groups would select its penalty against the labels it is about to be scored on and would still produce a plausible number. The inner folds are also required to be a partition rather than overlapping sets.

**Partition completeness** is what makes the two ends exact, and it is checked rather than assumed: every tensor of the checkpoint belongs to exactly one declared group, and an unclassified tensor name is refused, because one this function cannot place would be left out of every selection and the full transplant would stop being the donor.

## The compatibility census for the recommended pair

The handoff records three open questions about Galactica-1.3B against InstructProtein: whether the head counts agree, which no record on this host stated; that the embedding and head widths stand at 50,000 against 50,304 rows, so any partition must place them in their own groups and a census that finds a shape mismatch must refuse rather than resize; and whether the two checkpoints share an initialisation, which is declared nowhere, so that whether a transplant is even meaningful is a question a census decides. That census is CPU-only, needs no cohort and no forward pass. It ran in an H200 pod because that is where the checkpoints are staged, on four torch threads — a float64 reduction's order depends on the thread count, and four is also polite on a node whose CPU is shared between allocations — reading two checkpoints' configuration and all 1,315,201,024 stored elements, in one format each: the recipient as a single pickled state dict and the donor as sharded safetensors with an index.

**The head counts agree, at 32.** So do model type `opt`, hidden size 2048, feed-forward dimension 8192, depth 24 blocks, word-embedding projection dimension 2048, position budget 2048, and the four layer-norm and bias flags. Two fields differ.

| Field | Galactica-1.3B | InstructProtein | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `activation_function` | `gelu` | `relu` | a transplant is undefined |
| `vocab_size` | 50,000 | 50,304 | the embedding is shape-incompatible |

The activation difference is the finding the handoff's property set could not have carried: it records MLP structure as dense and ungated on both sides. Worse, the admitted census could not have carried it either — it compared the literal key `hidden_act`, which OPT does not use, so `config.get("hidden_act")` returned nothing on both sides and a gelu-against-relu pair read as *matching*. Installing feed-forward weights trained under one nonlinearity into a body that applies another is not a parameter transplant, and the census now refuses on it. The repair and its audit are the next section.

The vocabulary difference lands on exactly one tensor, and it lands there once rather than twice: neither config declares `tie_word_embeddings`, and the OPT family default ties input and output embeddings, so the embedding and the head are one intervention. `lm_head.weight` is stored in the recipient and absent from the donor, leaving 388 shared tensor names; `model.decoder.embed_tokens.weight` stands at [50000, 2048] against [50304, 2048] in float16 against float32, is recorded shape-incompatible, and its group is marked untransplantable so that a selection naming it raises instead of moving a truncated or padded tensor.

**No shared initialisation is evidenced, and this is the decisive result.** Element-difference counts cannot settle it — continued pretraining changed 98.905% of the ProLLaMA pair's elements and that pair does share an ancestor — so the census measures the Pearson correlation and the relative L2 distance between the two checkpoints' stored values, tensor by tensor. Two independent initialisations of *n* elements correlate at order `1 / sqrt(n)`, which is 5e-4 for a 2048×2048 projection.

| Group | Tensors | Parameters | Shape-compatible | Differing elements | Median Pearson *r* | Median relative L2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `embedding` | 1 | 102,400,000 | no | — | — | — |
| `positions` | 1 | 4,198,400 | yes | 4,196,019 | −0.0044 | 1.6284 |
| `norm` | 98 | 200,704 | yes | 200,704 | — | 0.5620 |
| `attention_q1`–`q4` | 48 each | 100,712,448 each | yes | 100,701,328–100,702,290 | −0.0001 to +0.0003 | 1.4842–1.5593 |
| `mlp_q1`–`q4` | 24 each | 201,388,032 each | yes | 201,366,018–201,373,914 | −0.0001 to +0.0001 | 1.5662–1.7659 |

The median correlation over the eight shape-compatible body groups is **1.26e-05**, and the relative L2 distances sit around the √2 ≈ 1.414 two independent unit-scale vectors give. The two checkpoints were trained independently. **Rank 5 is an architecture-matched comparison and no transplant is licensed on it**, on three independent grounds — the activation function, the tied shape-incompatible embedding, and the absence of a shared ancestor — and the handoff's conditional is now settled rather than open. Transplant work stays on the ProLLaMA lineage, and rank 5's value is what it always was on its own terms: a likelihood replication outside the Llama family at matched depth, width, architecture family and one-token-per-residue segmentation, and a depth-profile target.

## The repair the census forced, and how far the blind spot reached

A census whose purpose is to refuse incompatible pairs, and which cannot see a blocking incompatibility because two architectures spell the same field differently, will pass the next such pair for the same reason. The comparison is therefore now over **semantic fields** rather than key names: one declared table maps each field to the keys the families use, the first key a config declares wins, and a field neither config declares is reported as absent on both rather than silently reading as a match. The table and the comparison live in `capability_transplant.py`, where the defect was, so the transplant census and this interface share one definition instead of two.

How far the blind spot reached is measured rather than recalled. An audit stage resolves the table against fourteen staged checkpoints spanning eight declared model types, and **seven of fifteen fields are declared under more than one key across the panel**: the activation (`hidden_act` for the Llama, Mixtral, Qwen and ProGen3 arms, `activation_function` for the OPT, GPT-2 and ProGen2 arms, absent for RITA, ByGPT5 and ProteinGLM), the attention-head count (`num_attention_heads`, `n_head`, `num_heads`), the depth (`num_hidden_layers`, `n_layer`, `num_layers`), the hidden width (`hidden_size`, `n_embd`, `d_model`), the feed-forward width (`intermediate_size`, `ffn_dim`, `n_inner`, `d_ff`), the normalisation epsilon (`rms_norm_eps`, `layer_norm_epsilon`) and the position budget (`max_position_embeddings`, `n_positions`).

Applied pair by pair, that gives the extent of the old check's vacuity:

| Pair | Fields the single-key comparison could not see |
| --- | ---: |
| Llama-2-7B — ProLLaMA Stage 1 | 1, and it is `rope_scaling`, `None` on both sides under the same key |
| Galactica-1.3B — InstructProtein | 4, including the activation that differs |
| GPT-2-large — ProtGPT2 | **7**, including depth, hidden width, head count, normalisation epsilon, position budget and activation |

The admitted ProLLaMA census is therefore sound and its behaviour is unchanged by the repair, which is the one compatibility statement that had to hold and is asserted by test. The exposure was on the other two shape-compatible pairs, and it was worst on rank 2: for two GPT-2-family checkpoints the old comparison returned `architecture_equal: True` while comparing *none* of their depth, width, head count, feed-forward width, normalisation epsilon or activation, because every key it looked for is absent from a GPT-2 config. Two GPT-2 checkpoints of different depth and width would have passed it. That is asserted as a negative path rather than described: a synthetic GPT-2 pair differing at 36 against 48 blocks and 1280 against 1600 width produces no mismatch under the old key tuple and exactly `depth` and `hidden_width` under the semantic comparison.

**Both halves of this belong in the record together, so a reader is not left guessing which.** The defect was material: `architecture_record` returned `architecture_equal: True` for GPT-2-large against ProtGPT2 while comparing none of their depth, hidden width, head count, feed-forward width, normalisation epsilon or activation, so two GPT-2 checkpoints of different depth and width would have passed the compatibility gate that exists to refuse them. Seven of fifteen fields declared under more than one key across fourteen checkpoints is the general form of it. And the defect was **latent rather than a retraction**: the only pair the admitted census was ever run on is the ProLLaMA lineage, whose two configs declare every field under the keys the old tuple named, so its comparison resolved the same values and found the same nothing. That its behaviour is unchanged is asserted by test, and all twenty-one existing `tests/test_capability_transplant.py` tests pass against the repair. Nothing published on that pair needs revising; what needed repairing is what the gate would have admitted next.

The same function carried a second defect of the same shape. It refused a tied embedding pair with `if configs[0].get("tie_word_embeddings")`, so a config that *omits* the flag read as untied — and the transformers base configuration defaults it to `True`, which is why neither Galactica nor InstructProtein declares it and both are in fact tied. Omission now resolves to the family default, the refusing direction, and a pair of tied configs is refused where it was previously admitted.

## What the design licenses, and what it excludes

Carried in every cell's record rather than in prose alone, so a reader of one artefact cannot reach the recovered fraction without it.

A transplant that recovers a gain shows those parameter values are **sufficient in this context**: against this recipient, on this support, under this scoring. It does not show they are necessary — necessity is the complement cell, transplanting every group except one, and neither claim may be read off the other. It **does not identify a circuit** and says nothing about what the computation is; a recovered fraction is a parameter-space localisation.

And the parent and Stage 1 differ by continued pretraining, which fixed a corpus, a schedule, a data order and a token budget at once. Any localisation is therefore of **where that training's effect lands in the parameters**, not of an architectural locus. A pair differing in one declared property still confounds it with everything undeclared about the two training runs, and a pair differing in several cannot attribute an effect to any one of them.

Two further exclusions. At batch size one the repeat check's reference forward and the production forward are the same single-row computation, so a zero drift is true by construction and certifies nothing: a batch-one cell is internally consistent, not numerically validated, and the whole ProLLaMA lineage is batch-one. And ProGen3 is singleton-only — no kernel above float16 and a measured batch dependence of 0.02464 nats per scored token at batch size two — so both ProGen3 arms carry that bound and the panel's only bfloat16 extraction, which means any ProGen3-specific residual is confounded with the one precision deviation in the panel.

## The composition rule with the depth sweep

A pair is a sharper patching target when three conditions hold together. Both sides are among the 19 position-resolved arms, so a mutant-minus-wild-type difference at a residue index is defined on both and a residue-indexed intervention site exists. Their depths are equal, so block *j* on one side corresponds to block *j* on the other with no rescaling convention. And their per-block increment profiles differ at an identifiable block or contiguous band rather than only in the pooled two-depth endpoint. Under all three the intervention has a declared site — that block, those positions — and a declared prediction, that the increment at that block moves toward the donor's. A pooled endpoint supplies neither, which is why **a pair whose outcomes differ and whose depth profiles diverge at an identifiable block is a far sharper target than a pair differing only at the endpoint**.

Two degradations are stated so a later reader does not repair them by convention. Where depths differ, any block correspondence is a convention rather than a measurement: the comparison is between normalised depths *j*/(*L*−1), the site is a matched band rather than a matched index, and the convention must be recorded with the number. Where either side is among the 15 multi-residue arms, no position-resolved summary exists and none is defined, so the pair supports whole-block or parameter-group interventions only.

Applied to the six named pairs, reading their declared facts out of the handoff's own separation report rather than transcribing them, so the rule cannot disagree with the document that owns them:

| Pair | Depths | Both position-resolved | Equal depth | Outcome differs | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Llama-2-7B — ProLLaMA Stage 1 | 32 = 32 | no | yes | likelihood and representation | fails the first condition |
| GPT-2-large — ProtGPT2 | 36 = 36 | no | yes | likelihood and representation | fails the first condition |
| ProGen3-112M — ProtGPT3-1.3B | 10 ≠ 17 | yes | no | representation | degrades to a normalised band |
| ProGen3-3B — RITA-XL | 24 = 24 | yes | yes | representation | first two hold |
| Galactica-1.3B — InstructProtein | 24 = 24 | yes | yes | likelihood | first two hold |
| Galactica-6.7B — ProGen2-xlarge | 32 = 32 | yes | yes | likelihood and representation | first two hold |

**The ProLLaMA lineage fails the rule, and the recommended non-Llama pair satisfies it, which is what makes the two handoffs complementary rather than redundant.** Both sides of the ProLLaMA lineage are multi-residue — the measured segmentation runs 1.3043 to 1.7917 residues per token and it is one of the 15 arms for which no position-resolved depth summary is defined — so it supplies a parameter-group localisation and no residue-indexed site however the sweep comes out. Rank 5 supplies a residue-indexed site at matched depth and no transplant. Neither handoff can do the other's work.

Eleven of the 398 outcome-differing pairs satisfy the first two conditions; the third is left unevaluated until the depth sweep reports, and no pair is recorded as satisfying the rule on two conditions out of three.

## Which pairs are robust to the survival pattern changing

The 8-of-33 representation survival pattern is provisional, and the reassessment's first depth results place three arms above zero at previously unhooked blocks, which widens the survivor set rather than moving it.

**Robust: any pair whose statement rests on the likelihood boundary.** The gate reports the likelihood result as final — a likelihood enters a comparison as one scalar column, so the readout class barely bites on it, and no class sweep or depth re-extraction can move the 14-of-33 count. That covers the ProLLaMA lineage, whose whole purpose is the likelihood localisation; GPT-2-large / ProtGPT2, whose likelihood contrast stands at −0.01597 [−0.03564, +0.00493] against +0.33865 [+0.30871, +0.36565]; Galactica-1.3B / InstructProtein at +0.04754 [+0.02567, +0.06915] against +0.28372 [+0.25509, +0.31163]; and the likelihood half of Galactica-6.7B / ProGen2-xlarge at +0.08444 [+0.06207, +0.10641] against +0.39206 [+0.36438, +0.41839].

Rank 5 is the strongest case and the census does not weaken it. Its representation statement is a null on both sides, so recomputation can only leave it a likelihood pair, make it a representation pair as well if one side resolves, or invert it if the other does — all three informative — and since no transplant was ever licensed on it, a change in the representation pattern costs it nothing it had.

**Not robust: every statement resting on the representation side.** ProGen3-112M / ProtGPT3-1.3B rests on ProGen3-112M, whose individual increment is recorded only inside a two-arm group range of +0.01046 to +0.02487; ProGen3-3B / RITA-XL rests on a one-seed outcome difference that any recomputation moving RITA-XL's third seed would erase; and Galactica-6.7B / ProGen2-xlarge's representation half rests on ProGen2-xlarge remaining a survivor, which is the ladder rung most likely to move, since its admitted increment is the ladder's smallest at +0.00075 to +0.00647 against +0.00256 to +0.01341 for the other four. Its declared fallback substitutes ProGen2-large, which keeps the depth match at 32 blocks and loses the width match. GPT-2-large / ProtGPT2's representation half is fragile in the same way, at two of three seeds.

If the reassessment widens the survivor set substantially, the 8-of-33 boundary dissolves rather than moves, the whole family of representation pairs loses its subject, and only the likelihood pairs remain as targets — which is the argument for running rank 5's depth-profile comparison now and holding the representation pairs until the reassessment reports.

## Limitations

The census is a census of stored values. Differing tensors do not establish which values carry a behaviour, and identical tensors are a no-op transplant rather than evidence of insensitivity. The shared-initialisation verdict rests on a declared correlation threshold of 0.1; the measured 1.26e-05 is four orders of magnitude below it and the relative L2 distances agree independently, but the threshold is a declared boundary rather than a test with a size. The correlation is computed over stored weights at float64 accumulation under a pinned thread count, which makes it reproducible on its own terms and not guaranteed bit-identical on another thread budget.

The anchor's assay-id digest is not recorded on this host, so `check_support_identity` is only as strong as the digest a caller supplies: a mismatch against a manifest digest is caught, and a digest computed from the cell's own support passes by construction. The containment of the anchor's 201 assays in the ProLLaMA lineage's native 211 is unverified here. The semantic field table is a declared table of key aliases over the families this panel contains; a family it does not list resolves its fields to absent, which the comparison reports rather than hides, but the table has to be extended before that family is compared. The fold-identity spelling is declared in this module and reproduces the content the admitted fits digest under their own vocabularies; it is not the same function object as any of them, and the comparison is of content rather than of code.

No cell has been run. Every refusal above is exercised by test against synthetic checkpoints and synthetic census records, and the only real measurement in this document is the compatibility census. The interface therefore establishes that a mechanism result *would* compose; it establishes nothing about any mechanism.

## Artefacts, tests and entry points

`src/transfer/mechanistic_interface.py` holds the contract, the refusals, the OPT-aware partition and census, and the composition rule; `scripts/transfer/census_mechanistic_pair.py` runs its two stages; `tests/test_mechanistic_interface.py` holds the conditions that must always hold. Twenty-six tests pass, and the negative paths are the point of most of them: monkeypatching one module's split seeds makes the contract refuse; a 200- or 202-assay support and a 211-assay support are each refused; a wrong fold digest and a wrong fold count are refused; a held group placed in an inner fold raises by name, as do overlapping inner folds and an empty inner partition, while the admitted splitter at the admitted inner seed rule passes; a ceiling's digests offered as a floor, a missing tensor and an unexpected tensor are each refused; an endpoint outside its admitted interval is refused while an inexact one inside it is not; a byte-identical group, a shape-incompatible group and an undeclared group name are each refused by `group_provenance`; an unclassified OPT tensor, a duplicate name and an incomplete partition are refused; and the census is exercised end to end against four synthetic OPT checkpoint pairs — one with mismatched vocabulary, one drifted from a shared ancestor, one independently initialised and one with a differing activation function — so that the shape refusal, the shared-initialisation verdict in both directions, and the activation refusal each fire on real files. The composition rule is tested on all three conditions and both degradations, and the pending state is asserted to be pending rather than false. The repair carries its own regression tests: the activation resolves under either key, an omitted tie flag resolves to tied and makes `architecture_record` refuse where it previously admitted, a GPT-2 pair differing in depth and width is invisible to the old key tuple and caught by the semantic comparison, a Llama pair against itself produces no mismatch and no absent field so the admitted census is unchanged, and the audit names a field two families spell differently as blind while leaving one every family spells the same alone.
