# The local-context gate: qualifying a local control before using it

**Completed and admitted, 2026-09-24. The control qualification and the likelihood increments are final; the representation increments are provisional pending the programme's readout-class reassessment.** This document owns one independently qualified rung of the capability map: whether a local control that itself generalises across held-out wild-type clusters absorbs the model-side increment on the frozen Readout anchor. Four of six declared candidate local controls qualified and two were discarded; the qualified control is a stronger predictor of held-out clusters than the set it extends, at +0.47906 to +0.48290 Spearman against +0.44590 to +0.45316; a separate result of its own is that a predictor seeing only a seven-residue window around the mutation reaches about 90% of what the 444-column composition control reaches; and the native likelihood's increment over the qualified control survives at all three split seeds in all six protein-corpus arms. The representation increments are tabulated with their intervals but carry no verdict about representational content, because the readout class is under reassessment elsewhere in the programme.

The [crossed-controls study](D1_HIERARCHY_CROSSED_CONTROLS.md) attempted this rung with a declared short-range block `L` — 400 exact mutant-minus-wild-type dipeptide count differences plus a 256-coordinate fixed Gaussian projection of the 8,000 tripeptide count differences over the complete sequence — and that block failed as a control. It lowered the held-cluster Spearman of the control it augmented by 0.04747 from C and by 0.01684 from C+P, with the same sign at all three split seeds. A control that makes its own baseline worse bounds nothing, so the local-motif rung carries **no verdict** there, which is a different statement from a representation having exceeded it. This entry point starts again from the qualification requirement rather than from the block: **a local control may be used for a capability claim only after it has been shown to generalise across held-out clusters itself.**

## What this measures

The question is whether, on identical support, folds, weighting and experimental-label budget, adding a model's native likelihood or its frozen representation still raises held-cluster ranking of mutation effects once a *qualified* mutation-centred local control has been fitted — alone, over composition, and over composition plus mutation-local profile statistics. A positive increment identifies accessible predictive information beyond those declared controls under one fixed readout class. It locates no mechanism and establishes no irreducible biological rule at any level of sequence organisation.

Everything that determines a fitted number is inherited unchanged from the admitted [Readout diagnostic](D1_READOUT_PROTOCOL.md) through the same call sites the crossed-controls study uses: the frozen 201-assay / 163-cluster / 25,728-variant anchor with cohort SHA256 `4093ac34368cd9e7be02e5c84a22655ce78cb9bb4e4d58d626f281868dbe7992`, five deterministic outer and four inner wild-type-cluster folds at split seeds 20260923, 20260924 and 20260925, the fixed representation-projection seeds 20260923 through 20260926, within-assay standardized-rank targets, equal cluster and within-cluster assay weights, training-only weighted feature scaling, the ridge penalty grid with its tie rule, and 2,000 paired wild-type-cluster bootstrap draws at seed 20260923. The composition control C and the mutation-local profile block P are the crossed-controls blocks themselves, imported rather than reimplemented, so the C and C+P columns reproduce that study's values and every increment composes with its tables directly.

## Why the declared blocks are built differently

`L` counted specific k-mer identities over the whole sequence. Which particular dipeptide a substitution destroys is close to unique to one wild type, so a coefficient fitted on training clusters has almost nothing to carry to a held-out cluster, and 656 sparse columns spend penalty without buying transfer. The blocks declared here make the opposite choice on three axes at once: they read a bounded window around the mutated position rather than the whole sequence, they coarsen either the alphabet or the residue onto a scale rather than counting identities, and every coordinate is an average over window residues rather than a count of one event. A coefficient fitted on one family is then a statement about local chemistry rather than about one family's k-mer inventory.

## The declared block set

The declaration is frozen before any fit, with SHA256 `255b7e3d1e42893e9c05cd201e2ad9685015a24bc14b5aeefbdee102e5aa25c1` over its canonical JSON form; every cell report carries that digest and the collector refuses cells that disagree. Context radii are 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 residues. A window at radius *r* around a mutated position spans the *2r + 1* positions clipped to the sequence, and the three descriptor blocks read that window on the wild type with the mutated position removed, so what they express is the context of the substitution rather than the substitution, which C already carries. A variant's descriptor row is the mean over its substitutions.

| Candidate | Width | Content |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `wcomp` | 30 | Per radius: the four fractions of the repository's declared four-way chemical partition of the alphabet over the context window, that fraction vector's Shannon entropy in nats, and the fraction of the nominal 2*r* context positions lying inside the sequence |
| `wchem` | 66 | Per substitution: the change on each of six z-scored scales (Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy, formal charge at pH 7, side-chain volume, Grantham polarity, Chou–Fasman helix propensity, Chou–Fasman sheet propensity); per radius, each scale's context-window mean; and per radius, the product of the two |
| `wsub` | 15 | Per radius: the mean BLOSUM62 half-bit score of the mutant residue against every context-window residue, the same for the wild-type residue, and their difference |
| `wall` | 111 | The union of the three above |
| `rf3`, `rf7` | 280, 600 | The limited-receptive-field comparator at radii 3 and 7 |

Four scales come from the repository's existing declared tables; the two Chou–Fasman conformational parameters P(α) and P(β) are transcribed here from Chou and Fasman (1978), *Advances in Enzymology* 47:45–148, because local secondary-structure propensity is the one local-structural axis the other four do not carry and a local-motif control that omitted it would not express the thing the rung is named after. Each scale is z-scored over the twenty residues of its own fixed published table, so the six are commensurable under one shared ridge penalty and no cohort, corpus or label enters the standardisation. The position-independent BLOSUM62 score of the substitution itself is deliberately absent from `wsub`: it is a fixed function of the ordered residue pair and therefore already lies in the linear span of C's 400 directed substitution counts, so only its interaction with the neighbourhood is new. BLOSUM62 is estimated from aligned families, so `wsub` is local in the sense of reading a bounded window, not in the sense of using sequence arrangement alone.

`rf3` and `rf7` are the cleanest operationalisation of local information: a predictor that sees the bounded window around each mutated position and nothing else. Per substitution at position *i* the encoding is a positional one-hot over the twenty residues of the wild-type sequence at relative offsets −*r* through +*r*, concatenated with the mutant-minus-wild-type positional one-hot difference at the same offsets; offsets outside the sequence contribute zero rows. No absolute position, no sequence length, no composition outside the windows, no alignment or profile quantity and no model output reaches these columns. The two halves span the same space as the two states' one-hots, and this basis is the declared one because the difference half is sparse — for a single substitution it carries exactly one +1 and one −1, at the centre offset — which separates the context the window supplies from the substitution events inside it, and because a ridge penalty is not invariant to that choice. Pooling over a variant's substitutions is a **sum** rather than a mean, which is what a bounded-receptive-field encoder does and which keeps the substitution count inside the window description: the wild-type half's total mass is the number of in-sequence window positions. The comparator therefore does see co-substitutions that fall inside a window, where the three descriptor blocks, which read the wild-type window, do not.

## The qualification gate

The gate is a refusal, declared before fitting and applied to control-versus-control correlations only. A candidate qualifies when, **at every one of the three split seeds**, adding it does not lower the held-cluster Spearman of C and does not lower that of C+P. Exactly zero is not a refusal; one negative seed is, however large the other two are, because a control that makes its own baseline worse at any declared split cannot bound anything. A limited-receptive-field comparator carries one further requirement: its own held-cluster correlation must be above zero with a 95% cluster-bootstrap interval excluding zero at every split seed, because a comparator that does not predict held-out clusters on its own is not a competitor to anything.

Among qualified descriptor candidates the primary local control is the one with the largest mean increase of the C baseline over the three split seeds; a qualified comparator is carried separately, because a bounded-window predictor and a descriptor block are different kinds of object. Both selection quantities are control-versus-control and read no model output, so a candidate cannot be tuned against the increment it is later meant to absorb. Candidates that fail are discarded and reported as discarded; none is carried forward.

The qualification stage fits no model quantity at all. Its cells load the anchor arm's artifacts for the support and the labels and discard that arm's likelihood and representation, and the collector refuses a receipt whose cells report any fitted model column.

## Most of what the composition control reaches is available inside a seven-residue window

This is a result about the cohort rather than about any model, and it is the clearest thing the comparator establishes.

Fitted alone on held-out wild-type clusters, `rf3` — a predictor that sees the seven residues centred on each mutated position in both states and nothing else — reaches **+0.27559, +0.27076 and +0.27380** Spearman at the three split seeds, with intervals [+0.24053, +0.30991], [+0.23579, +0.30355] and [+0.23866, +0.30722]. The admitted 444-column composition control, which sees the mutation's identity, its absolute position, the mutation count, both states' whole-sequence residue frequencies and the sequence length, reaches +0.30024, +0.29725 and +0.30194. The bounded window therefore recovers 91.8%, 91.1% and 90.7% of the composition control's held-cluster ranking from 280 columns that carry no absolute position, no sequence length and nothing outside the windows. The 111-column descriptor union `wall` reaches +0.25613, +0.25981 and +0.25498, which is 85.3%, 87.4% and 84.4%.

Two readings follow. Mutation-effect ranking on this cohort is, to about nine parts in ten of what composition-level descriptors reach, a function of the mutation's immediate neighbourhood — so the composition control's strength is itself substantially local, and a control described as "composition and length" is not thereby free of local information. And because `rf3` adds only +0.00082 to +0.00871 to C+P while reaching +0.27 on its own, most of what the bounded window supplies is also supplied by the mutation-local profile statistics: the profile control's apparent strength is in large part local information expressed through alignment columns rather than information of a different kind. Neither reading says that longer-range information is absent; it says that under this readout class, at this label budget, the accessible part of it beyond a seven-residue window is small.

Local context, encoded densely and read at a bounded radius, is therefore a real predictor of mutation effect across held-out families on this cohort, which is exactly what the earlier dipeptide and tripeptide difference block failed to establish.

## Full-panel generalizability check

The nine-arm analysis above remains the primary declared measurement. Its arm panel was chosen to cover the interface strata and it includes the three arms the admitted Readout study had already reported as its only resolved positive increments, so the arm-level pattern in it cannot be read as independent. To test whether that pattern is a property of the panel or an artifact of the selection, the measurement stage was re-run across **all 33 unconditioned admitted Readout arms** at the same three split seeds — 99 cells in total, with the frozen gate receipt and the carried-forward blocks unchanged and **not** re-qualified against the wider panel, so the qualification's outcome-blindness is preserved. ZymCTRL is the one admitted arm excluded: its EC-conditioned 40-assay panel is a different support, and this entry point fits the anchor only, so the roster is 33 of the 34 admitted arms.

All 72 new cells exited 0, the 27 cells of the primary analysis were reused unchanged, and the 99-cell admission (`local_context_measurement.json`, SHA256 `6d18d2f40c99e6c969121d0806794c8dd4017af4ef3bc344093e7014b160447e`) verified the same bindings, including identical fitting-code hashes against the gate receipt and bit-identical predictions for all eight model-independent designs at each seed. The control correlations are identical across all 33 arms with spread 0.0 and equal to the values tabulated above.

**The likelihood result generalises, and it sharpens.** The native likelihood's increment over C+P+`wall` is resolved above zero at all three split seeds in **14 of the 33 arms**, and those 14 are exactly the arms pretrained on protein sequence: the five ProGen2 sizes, both ProGen3 sizes, ProteinGLM-7B-CLM, ProtGPT2, ProtGPT3-1.3B, RITA-XL, InstructProtein and both ProLLaMA stages. Two further arms reach one resolved seed (Galactica-30B and Qwen2.5-32B). Exactly one arm has any seed resolved below zero (ByGPT5-base-en, at one seed) and none is below zero at all three. The four Galactica sizes and the Llama-2-7B parent — text-corpus checkpoints reading protein — produce no resolved increment at any seed, and neither does any literal-text arm. This is the final reading: **a qualified mutation-centred local control, the composition descriptors and mutation-local profile statistics together do not account for the mutation-effect information in the native likelihood of a protein-pretrained model, and they fully account for it in every model without protein pretraining.**

**The nine-arm representation pattern does not generalise as an interface property, and this is why the check was worth running.** Over C+P+`wall` the representation increment is resolved above zero at all three seeds in 8 of the 33 arms: ProGen2 at all five sizes (+0.01006 to +0.01667), ProGen3 at both (+0.01046 to +0.02487) and ProteinGLM-7B-CLM (+0.02605 to +0.03033). Two more reach two of three seeds, ProtGPT2 (+0.00752 to +0.01431) and RITA-XL (+0.00911 to +0.01399). Critically, RITA-XL, ProtGPT3-1.3B and InstructProtein are protein-corpus arms that emit exactly one token per residue and do **not** survive at all three seeds, so the nine-arm reading — that the residual belongs to protein-residue-tokenizer interfaces — is **not** supported by the panel. What the eight all-seed survivors share is narrower than an interface class: they are the ProGen2, ProGen3 and ProteinGLM checkpoints specifically. No claim is made about why, and the whole representation side remains provisional pending the readout-class reassessment.

The segmentation reading strengthens on the panel and keeps its sign. None of the 15 arms whose measured segmentation is multi-residue has a representation increment resolved above zero at all three seeds, against 8 of the 18 one-token-per-residue arms — but 10 of those 18 do not survive either, so one token per residue is necessary in this panel and far from sufficient. Segmentation therefore still fails to explain the pattern, in the direction the nine-arm analysis found.

## Gate verdict

The verdict divides by what the readout class can and cannot affect, and only the first part is stated as settled.

**Final: the control qualifies, and the likelihood increment survives it.** For the local-motif rung, mutation-centred local context encoded densely at radii 1 to 16 residues qualifies as a control: it generalises across held-out wild-type clusters on its own at +0.25498 to +0.25981 Spearman, its own contribution to both baselines is resolved above zero at every split seed, and C+P+`wall` at +0.47906 to +0.48290 predicts held-out clusters better than the C+P it extends at +0.44590 to +0.45316. That is the thing the crossed-controls study could not obtain, and it does not depend on the readout class: the qualification, the two discards with their magnitudes, and the selection are all control-versus-control fits of sequence and alignment statistics.

Over that qualified control the **native likelihood's** increment is above zero with a resolved interval at all three seeds in all six protein-corpus arms of the primary panel — +0.02328 to +0.02503 (ProGen3-3B), +0.02340 to +0.02445 (ProteinGLM), +0.01951 to +0.02097 (ProGen2-base), +0.01477 to +0.01570 (ProtGPT2), +0.00280 to +0.00348 (ProLLaMA Stage 1), +0.00356 to +0.00440 (Stage 2) — and within 0.0002 of zero at every seed for the Llama-2-7B parent, Galactica-125M and ByGPT5-small-en. Across all 33 arms it holds in 14 of 33, which are exactly the 14 protein-pretrained arms. A likelihood enters as one scalar column, so the readout-class question barely bites on it, and this is reported as a standing result: **mutation-centred local context, the composition descriptors and mutation-local profile statistics together do not account for the mutation-effect information in a protein-pretrained model's native likelihood, and they do account for all of it in every model without protein pretraining.**

**Provisional: the representation increments.** The representation tables above are reported with their intervals, but as provisional and **conditional on the fixed compressed linear readout class at the two retained depths** — 1,024 fixed Gaussian projection coordinates of four pooled hidden-state summaries under linear ridge on the admitted penalty grid. A readout-class sweep and a full-depth re-extraction are running elsewhere in the programme, and their outcome determines whether an arm showing no residual over C+P+`wall` lacks the information in its states or merely lacks a readout able to reach it. No verdict sentence about what any model's representations contain is drawn here. In particular no interface account of the residual is asserted: the primary panel's apparent concentration in protein-residue-tokenizer arms is already contradicted on the full panel, where RITA-XL, ProtGPT3-1.3B and InstructProtein emit one token per residue on protein corpora and do not survive at all three seeds. These numbers are subject to recomputation.

**Segmentation is ruled out as an explanation of the measured pattern.** This closes a confound the crossed-controls work left open, and it holds regardless of which way the readout reassessment goes, because it is a statement about the association between two measured quantities rather than about representational content. On the full 33-arm panel, none of the 15 arms whose measured segmentation is multi-residue has a representation increment resolved above zero at all three seeds, while 8 of the 18 one-token-per-residue arms do — and 10 of those 18 do not, so one token per residue is necessary in this panel and far from sufficient. Had a segmentation degree of freedom been the mechanism, the increment would have concentrated in the arms carrying a nonzero token-count cycle on every variant at 1.30 to 4.98 residues per token; instead those arms carry none of the all-seed survivors, and the four Galactica sizes and three ByGPT5 sizes show that one token per residue on its own buys nothing. Whatever the surviving increments turn out to reflect, segmentation is not it.

A surviving increment, provisional or not, is bounded by the declared controls, the experimental-label budget of 163 wild-type clusters and the readout class stated above. It identifies accessible predictive information beyond those controls on held-out wild-type clusters. **It locates no mechanism**, does not say which computation carries the information, does not establish an irreducible biological rule at any level of sequence organisation, and does not show that the information is local or non-local in any sense beyond "not reachable from these declared local descriptors under this readout".

## Conditions the tests enforce

`tests/test_local_context_gate.py` covers the four conditions that have to hold for any number here to be readable, plus the exactness and the refusals of the declared blocks. Twenty-one tests pass.

The gate itself is tested as a negative path. A candidate whose augmentation lowers the C baseline at one seed, or the C+P baseline at one seed, or either by 10⁻⁹, must be refused and must not appear in the carried-forward set; a candidate whose deltas are exactly zero at all three seeds must be admitted; a comparator whose own held-cluster interval includes zero must be refused even when both its deltas are positive; and incomplete evidence — a missing split seed, a missing measured quantity, a point estimate that is undefined — must raise rather than be scored.

Identical support, folds and seeds against the crossed-controls fits. The same rows handed to both evaluations at one split seed must produce identical outer and inner group membership, identical assay, cluster and variant counts, bit-identical predictions and identically selected penalties for the eight designs the two studies share, and identical bootstrap summaries for their shared increments.

No held-out group label reaching feature construction, feature scaling or tuning. Replacing the measured effects of every group held out in one outer fold with arbitrary values must leave that fold's held-out predictions and its selected penalty unchanged for every design, and the fold records must have no family in both the held and training sets and no inner validation family drawn from the held set. Feature construction takes the wild type, the mutation strings and the retained mutant sequences only, so a label cannot enter a column even in principle.

The comparator genuinely sees only its window. Changing any residue outside every mutated position's window must leave its rows unchanged; changing any residue inside a window must change them; the same window content placed at a different absolute position in a sequence of different length must give an identical row; and the wild-type half's mass must equal the number of in-sequence window positions.

The block tests check the window class fractions, entropy and coverage against a brute-force recount, the chemistry block's substitution change and context means against the declared z-scored tables, the substitution block against BLOSUM62 evaluated over the window, and the comparator against a hand-built window. Every block refuses a length-changing variant, a wild-type residue that disagrees with the mutation string, a repeated or out-of-range position, a noncanonical residue and a mutant sequence that does not match its mutation string, through the one shared substitution validator.

## Limitations

The readout class is one fixed compressed linear family — linear ridge on the admitted penalty grid, with the representation entering as 1,024 fixed label-independent Gaussian projection coordinates of four pooled hidden-state summaries — so a null or negative increment is a statement about that projected linear class at this label budget rather than about the information in the uncompressed features. The declared radii stop at 16 residues and the comparator's at 7, so nothing here speaks to a longer-range local pattern. The descriptor blocks read the wild-type window, so co-substitutions inside a window are invisible to them. `wsub` uses a family-derived substitution matrix and is therefore not free of evolutionary statistics. The grouping unit is the existing single-linkage wild-type cluster at 50% identity with 80% coverage of the shorter sequence; it is not a certified Pfam-family or CATH-superfamily holdout and does not exclude overlap with pretraining corpora. The intervals are exploratory percentile intervals conditional on the fitted cross-validation predictions, omit training and tuning variation, and are not adjusted across candidates, arms, control sets, endpoints or split seeds. The three split seeds give a sensitivity range on one biological cohort, not independent replication.

Six further bounds belong with the outcome. The gate's refusal rule is a statement about a declared block under this readout class, so discarding `wsub` and `rf7` does not show that substitution compatibility in context or a 15-residue window carry no information — only that these encodings of them do not leave both baselines no worse off at every seed. The declaration was frozen before any fit and the selection rule reads only control-versus-control correlations, but the candidate set itself was chosen by design reasoning about why the earlier block failed, so the qualification is not outcome-blind with respect to that failure. The nine-arm primary panel is a deliberate stratum-covering selection that includes the three arms the admitted Readout study already reported as its only resolved positive increments, so its arm-level pattern is an extension of an inspected result rather than an independent confirmation; the 33-arm run is reported as its generalizability check and does not inherit that selection, but it is a re-reading of the same inspected cohort and roster and is not outcome-blind either. Five arms of the roster carry the expansion's structurally vacuous batch-one requalification, which bounds any number derived from their extractions, and both ProGen3 arms are singleton-only by their own numerical bound. The representation increments are conditional on the readout class and subject to recomputation, so every count of resolved arms on that side is provisional; the likelihood counts are not. And the surviving increments are small in absolute terms, on both sides and against a control correlation of +0.47906 to +0.48290: the native likelihood's increment over C+P+`wall` spans +0.00280 to +0.02503 Spearman across the six protein-corpus arms of the primary panel, and the representation increment over the same control spans +0.01006 to +0.03033 across the eight arms that survive at all three seeds on the full panel. Each range is the span of the per-arm point estimates tabulated above, not an interval. So the increments constrain what the declared controls reach rather than establishing a large capability difference.
