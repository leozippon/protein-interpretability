# Explaining the Direction-1 capability differences

This follow-up connects mutation ranking, native generation and homolog-context sensitivity through controlled interventions and incremental-prediction analyses. Its purpose is to distinguish plausible explanations of the existing capability differences, not to turn checkpoint correlations into a general law or to claim a biological mechanism before one is identified. The [canonical audit](INTERPRETABILITY_TRANSFER_AUDIT.md) remains authoritative for existing findings, retractions and scientific scope. The new protocols below govern these exploratory experiments; this document explains their relationship and summarizes admitted results.

## What the existing evidence motivates

The existing mutation comparisons already distinguish model likelihood from an explicit external sequence-profile baseline. The current ProGen3 comparison uses the R242 residue-only scored-span repair; older aggregates include a superseded scoring convention. The external profile is a specified baseline, not an exhaustive account of evolutionary information or a certified reconstruction of each model's training corpus. Consequently, a positive MODEL−LOOKUP correlation difference establishes an advantage over that baseline on its stated support, while a negative difference does not establish that model scores contain no complementary information.

The earlier homolog-context experiments measure likelihood and paired concordance against unrelated context. They demonstrate or fail to demonstrate effects on sequence scoring under declared packed interfaces; they do not directly evaluate experimental mutation effects. Their low-local-overlap controls reduce one copying explanation without identifying a parameter mechanism. Connecting these experiments to the same ProteinGym mutations therefore addresses a missing link between two existing endpoints.

The canonical audit records a ProLLaMA Stage 1→Stage 2 mutation-ranking increase alongside reduced unconditional profile recognition. It also records that nonempty and exact-unique generation counts alone did not explain that contrast. This is an informative within-lineage observation, but the bare sequence prompt, length budget, termination and recognition stages still need separation. Other checkpoint associations do not establish universal decoupling: mutation ranking and generation recognition can co-vary in a selected panel while individual stage transitions differ.

Earlier work also showed why plausible biological interpretations need stronger controls. In the preliminary fold-discordant sequence experiment, a profile-HMM control recruited structure-related sequences and removed the original contrast between a fragment-statistics explanation and a structural interpretation. The lesson for this follow-up is to test the relevant profile baseline directly, rather than treating a gain over a weaker comparison as evidence for a uniquely biological computation. This does not reopen the stopped preliminary experiment.

## Questions and identifying contrasts

| Question | Main comparison | What an informative result would establish | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Does supplied homolog context improve mutation ranking? | Within-assay homolog minus matched-unrelated Spearman, accompanied by WT likelihood changes and no-context scores | A change in fitness-relevant ordering caused by the supplied context under this interface | Rescue does not establish that the corresponding information was absent from the weights; failure does not exclude another context format or selection policy |
| Does the model add predictive information beyond the profile? | Partial rank correlation and family-held-out profile-plus-model versus profile-only prediction | Complementary predictive information relative to the retained scalar profile score and fitted predictor | A residual is not a parameter mechanism; incomplete or nonlinear profile controls can leave residual association |
| Under which observed conditions does relative performance deteriorate? | Family-level MODEL−LOOKUP against homolog depth, identity, entropy and sequence length | Associations between measured data conditions and relative predictive performance | These are observational covariates of the searched corpus; depth censoring, correlated predictors and unmeasured conditions prevent causal attribution |
| Which generation stages account for reduced recognition? | All-attempt format, stop, completeness, profile and nonredundancy outcomes, with a residue-budget confirmation | Whether the observed reduction coincides with formatting, termination, budget censoring, repetition or recognition of completed products | A Pfam hit is not measured folding or function; an unrecognized sequence is not necessarily biologically invalid |
| Which Stage 1/2 components carry the paired changes? | A tensor inventory, contemporaneous native endpoints and a bounded attention/MLP factorial over distinct configurations | Effects of exchanging components that actually differ between these checkpoints, including identifiable interactions | Exchanging identical tensors is a no-op, not a causal null; hybrid incompatibility can damage behavior, and a component effect does not identify a circuit or explain training |

Positive homolog-driven WT likelihood changes alongside an unresolved ranking contrast would separate the measured endpoints, but would not establish a zero ranking effect. A useful distinction requires the effect estimate and uncertainty for both quantities; equivalence needs a meaningful margin declared before an equivalence test. Similarly, a model can correlate strongly with the profile and still add predictive information, or outperform LOOKUP in average correlation while adding little under a particular held-out predictor. The two profile analyses answer different questions and must both remain visible.

For the component experiment, the two native reconstructions must first reproduce their unmodified checkpoints, and a source-bound inventory must establish which component tensors differ. The nominal factorial is conditional on that inventory: configurations that contain exactly the same tensors represent one intervention, not independent experimental cells. An exchange of identical tensors cannot test the causal contribution of that component; its unchanged output is a no-op, not evidence that the component is dispensable. Identity must be established by the admitted inventory rather than inferred from a training-stage description or unchanged behavior.

For components that differ, both exchange directions and the identifiable interactions are needed to distinguish a component-associated effect from damage at a cross-stage interface. If mutation and generation outcomes move with different changed components, the evidence supports component-level separation in this pair. If only the body differs while embedding and head are identical, the endpoint contrast localizes the parameter changes to the body by construction; repeating identical interface swaps cannot independently establish a mechanism or the absence of an interface contribution. Opposing mutation and generation changes in that case remain a paired behavioral observation whose responsible computation is unresolved.

## Analysis and evidence boundaries

The [context-rescue protocol](D1_CONTEXT_RESCUE_PROTOCOL.md) freezes the diagnostic mutation draw, fragment selection, matched contexts, scoring spans and precision checks. The [profile-increment protocol](D1_PROFILE_INCREMENT_PROTOCOL.md) governs residual analysis, family-held-out prediction and historical failure-condition regressions. The [generation diagnostic protocol](D1_GENERATION_DIAGNOSTIC_PROTOCOL.md) governs attempt accounting, residue-budget confirmation and component interventions. Numerical recipes belong in those protocols rather than being duplicated here.

The new mutation diagnostic is a label-blind subsample of existing assay draws, not an independent biological replication. Context-feasible support can differ by tokenizer and is narrower than full historical support. Report each arm's retained assays, variants and families, every exclusion, and any explicitly constructed common-support comparison. Retain mutation-level scores and identities so profile analyses use precisely the same rows. Family averages and family-level resampling prevent large assays or closely related wild types from dominating inference; the retained identity clustering does not guarantee disjoint remote superfamilies.

Generation confirmation is smaller than the existing attempt census. Preserve every attempted output and distinguish an accepted native-format product from a censored prefix, even when both have a profile hit. A residue stopping budget reduces a tokenizer confound but does not force identical output lengths or equal termination probabilities. The Stage 2 bare sequence interface remains a specified operating point outside its supervised instruction prompt; it cannot support a claim of general generation loss.

These follow-ups were motivated by inspected results and are exploratory. Report all declared contrasts, undefined statistics, precision failures and incomplete cells. Do not select a favorable checkpoint, covariate, stratum or component after reading outcomes. Intervals must identify their resampling unit and what uncertainty they omit; pointwise intervals across many exploratory contrasts do not provide family-wise error control. Biological consequences on new, independently sourced families remain a separate confirmation question.

## Execution and results

All five authorized follow-up groups have completed their bounded H200 computation and evidence admission: context rescue, mutation-level profile increments, historical failure conditions, generation diagnostics and the revised component experiment. The original component factorial was reduced using exact tensor identity, then refined to distinct attention/MLP interventions. Computational completion does not mean that a biological mechanism or general capability separation has been established.

The [context-rescue results](D1_CONTEXT_RESCUE_RESULTS.md) distinguish three outcomes. Both ProLLaMA stages improve mutation ranking over homolog-matched unrelated context and over no context, including the low-local-overlap stratum, but remain below LOOKUP. ProGen3-3B improves over unrelated context while remaining below its no-context ranking: the primary gain recovers part of a prefix-induced loss. ProteinGLM increases WT likelihood while its mutation-ranking contrast remains unresolved. Native and exact shared mutation supports retain these interpretations; tokenizer-specific contexts and nats per scored token remain distinct. Low selected-homolog identity strata contain only three to six families across the native/shared analyses, so their intervals are withheld; the joint low-identity/low-overlap stratum contains one family.

The [mutation-level profile analyses](D1_PROFILE_INCREMENT_RESULTS.md#mutation-level-profile-increments) establish complementary prediction relative to the specified scalar profile for ProGen3-3B and ProteinGLM without supplied context, and for both ProLLaMA stages under homolog context. Positive partial rank association accompanies held-family improvements in Spearman and standardized-rank mean squared error in these conditions. A standalone MODEL−LOOKUP deficit therefore does not exclude complementarity. These fitted predictors and rank residuals do not control every nonlinear profile relationship or identify the computation responsible; the cross-validation intervals also condition on fitted predictions and omit training-set variation.

The historical failure-condition analysis completed on H200 on 2026-09-23; its [admitted results and provenance](D1_PROFILE_INCREMENT_RESULTS.md) retain the complete model table and covariate analyses. ProteinGLM's family-equal MODEL−LOOKUP Spearman difference is +0.0796 [+0.0303, +0.1278] in the low-depth stratum (58 assays / 46 families), compared with +0.0105 [−0.0153, +0.0370] in the high-depth stratum (77 assays / 59 families). Its adjusted depth coefficient is −0.0343 [−0.0688, −0.0048] difference per standard deviation of log10 effective homolog depth (201 assays / 163 family observations). These are exploratory 95% family-bootstrap intervals. Absolute model correlation increases with depth, while the profile strengthens more; the result concerns relative predictive advantage.

ProGen3-3B exceeds LOOKUP in each depth stratum, but its adjusted depth coefficient remains unresolved. ProGen3-112M's deficit is concentrated among shorter proteins: MODEL−LOOKUP is −0.1762 [−0.2412, −0.1137] in the short stratum (60 assays / 60 families) and −0.0001 [−0.0464, +0.0450] in the long stratum (77 assays / 57 families), with the same dimensionless endpoint and interval procedure. These associations refine the failure conditions without identifying an internal computation.

The historical nearest-hit identity distribution cannot provide a balanced remote-homology analysis: its tertile cut points are 99.7768% and 100% query-normalized identity, and the middle stratum has only four assays / three families, so its interval is withheld. Effective depth is also censored by the search hit cap. The selected context homologs answer a different identity question. The context and mutation-vector results above retain this distinction rather than treating the historical nearest-hit distribution as the selected-context identity distribution.

The [generation diagnostic results](D1_GENERATION_DIAGNOSTIC_RESULTS.md) admit all four 64-attempt residue-budget confirmations and four 800-attempt historical decompositions. In the new ProLLaMA Stage 2 draw, 59/64 attempts terminate in native format (92.2%, Wilson 95% interval [83.0%, 96.6%]), but 54/64 are complete without Pfam (84.4% [73.6%, 91.3%]); no empty or invalid-format output occurs (each 0/64, 0.0% [0.0%, 5.7%]). All new attempts form separate near-duplicate groups. This localizes the observed low recognition mainly to completed, unrecognized products under the bare sequence interface, without establishing their biological invalidity. Historical terminal absence remains unknown stopping because exact token traces were not retained. Changed seeds and stopping implementation prevent treating old/new recognition differences as a causal budget effect; the small confirmation alone does not resolve the Stage 1/2 recognition contrast.

The same generation report now includes a descriptor census of completed products. Stage 2 produces shorter terminated sequences in both the historical and confirmation cohorts; within-sequence repetition measures change in mixed directions. These descriptive, length-dependent differences do not isolate shortening, composition or repetition as the cause of reduced profile recognition, and a short normally terminated product is not thereby biologically incomplete.

The [completed component diagnostic](D1_COMPONENT_DIAGNOSTIC_RESULTS.md) establishes that Stage 1 and Stage 2 embeddings and LM heads have identical native and FP32 tensor values. The eight nominal combinations therefore contain only two distinct parameter configurations, represented by native endpoints `000` and `111`; these are exact inventory counts, not sampling estimates. The six redundant hybrid evaluations were omitted. Exchanging the unchanged interface is a no-op, not evidence of its causal irrelevance. Both native endpoints and a bounded attention/MLP refinement are now admitted.

The new native mutation draw yields a Stage 2-minus-Stage 1 family-equal Spearman difference of +0.0113 [−0.0023, +0.0266] on 217 assays / 174 families / 27,711 variants (dimensionless; exploratory 95% paired-family-bootstrap interval). It does not resolve the historical mutation gain on this smaller variant draw. In the refinement, mutation outcomes use 32 selected families / 4,096 variants; no mutation component contrast is resolved. The MLP exchange's marginal effect on all-attempt Pfam recognition is −8.59 percentage points [−17.19, −0.78] across four cells with 64 attempts each (exploratory unadjusted 95% interval from eight jointly resampled seed batches). Conditional recognition effects remain uncertain and completion shows an interaction. This supplies a bounded generation intervention effect, not identification of a mutation-gain component or of capability separation. Full contrasts, native-versus-panel supports and incompatibility limits remain in the detailed report.
