# Structure instrument for predicted folding confidence: reconciliation and qualification

Every structure-derived number in this programme carried a standing qualification: the current ESMFold2 outcomes were reported complete while the local checkout held none of the rows, calibration records or runtime receipts they rest on. This document owns the reconciliation of that gap and the resulting scope for the structure rung of the Direction-1 capability hierarchy. The [generation-biology preregistration](D1_GENERATION_BIOLOGY_PREREGISTRATION.md) owns the design, the predictor settings and the calibration rule; the [expansion protocol](D1_UNCONDITIONAL_GENERATION_EXPANSION.md) owns the seventeen-arm cohort. Nothing here is a model-capability finding: it is the qualification of a measuring instrument, in the same role as the [label instrument](D1_PAIRWISE_LABEL_INSTRUMENT.md) for the pairwise rung.

The gap had three named parts — missing row-level predictions, missing calibration records, missing receipts — and they turned out to have three different causes and three different remedies. The rows were a recovery problem, the calibration a scope problem, and the receipts a genuine defect in the code that wrote them.

## Decision

The structure instrument is **admitted with a narrower scope than the reported numbers imply**, and the row-level and receipt parts of the qualification are cleared. The licensed claim is a paired within-sequence contrast: predictor-assigned CA-pLDDT of a generated or natural sequence minus that of its own fixed composition shuffle, in pLDDT points, with uncertainty at the declared resampling unit. Three restrictions are part of the decision rather than caveats to it. The instrument does not repeat exactly: re-folding eight recorded rows at the same settings and seed reproduces their mean CA-pLDDT to 0.081 points at the median and 0.929 points at the worst of eight, so a single row's value carries about a point of repeat uncertainty that no recorded interval includes. Calibration is attained on the natural controls of two arms and inherited by the other seventeen, so a positive contrast on an inherited arm is evidence that the predictor responds to residue order in that arm's outputs, not evidence that the predictor was shown to separate natural from shuffled sequences in that arm's own sequence regime. And 56 of 6,762 admitted rows were folded with the 32-sample replica axis split into several chunks, which draws from a different sampling stream than an undivided call; the split costs 0.164 points at the median of eight paired measurements. The repeat spread and the split cost are both one to two orders of magnitude below the reported arm contrasts of +6.115 to +65.195 points, which is what admits the contrast while leaving a single row's value unfit to be quoted on its own.

## What was reconciled

All row-level artifacts were recovered from the remote store into the local checkout under `results/transfer/generation_evidence/structure/esmfold2_20260919155754/` (EXP-R2-232 pilot and main, EXP-R2-233 native) and `esmfold2_20260920012718/` (the seventeen expansion arms), together with the analysis trees under `results/transfer/generation_evidence/analysis/`. The compact verification record is `evidence/structure_instrument_20260923/structure_instrument_reconciliation.json` (SHA-256 `0c06575ee253726597859774d603c493ef7ea90e9c6d6caf6ae17e5257259df4`); the raw per-cell outputs stay in the ignored `logs/structure_reconcile/`.

The recovered evidence is 6,790 prediction rows over 46 shard cells and 20 pooled cohorts: 116 calibration rows, 2,320 main rows, 256 native rows and 4,098 expansion rows. Of these, 6,762 carry status `ok` and 28 carry `failed`; no row is pending, and no failure was dropped from a denominator.

## Row-level agreement

Nothing at row level disagrees, in three independent senses.

The stored per-residue CA array reproduces each row's own summary. Recomputing the mean and the fraction of residues at CA-pLDDT ≥ 70 from the stored array leaves 0 of 6,762 `ok` rows outside a tolerance of 1e-4 pLDDT points on the mean and 1e-6 (dimensionless) on the fraction.

The pooled artifact each analysis consumed is exactly the union of the shard indexes behind it. Comparing row identity, sequence digest, status, mean CA-pLDDT, evaluation signature and claimed file digests, all 20 pooled cohorts are row-identical to their shard indexes: 6,790 pooled rows against 6,790 shard rows, with 0 rows present on only one side.

Re-running the analysis locally reproduces every reported estimate. All 20 analysis cells were replayed in the `ct` environment on Compute with `scripts/transfer/analyze_generation_biology.py` at the recorded phase, primary condition and resampling unit, against inputs verified byte-for-byte against the `input_sha256` each analysis recorded. All 20 replayed; none was refused. Across the 22 arm estimates the largest absolute difference is **0.000 pLDDT points** on the arm mean and on both bounds of every reported interval, with identical prediction-status counts, identical structural-cell counts and identical interpretation labels.

One row-level fact was not visible anywhere before this reconciliation. The realized replica chunking is heterogeneous within cohorts: 6,706 of 6,762 `ok` rows come from one undivided 32-sample call, while 4 rows split as 16+16, 42 as four chunks of 8 and 10 as eight chunks of 4. All 56 split rows are OOM consequences on sequences of 716 to 954 residues (the six ZymCTRL calibration controls) or on the longest ProtGPT2 and ProLLaMA-Stage-1 outputs. The split is declared admissible by the preregistration, but no tree summary reported it, so a reader could not tell that a cohort mixed the two sampling streams. The repaired index summary now carries the distribution.

## Calibration

Calibration is attained, on 116 of 116 admitted calibration rows and with no row refused. Both natural-control arms clear the preregistered rule — a positive lower 95% class-bootstrap bound on the arm's mean natural-minus-own-shuffle CA-pLDDT difference — at ProLLaMA **+54.599 [+47.555, +60.535]** and ZymCTRL **+65.195 [+62.750, +67.201]** pLDDT points (seed 20260905, 4,000 resamples). Both values reproduce to 0.000 points in the local replay.

The scope restriction is in who inherits that attainment. The rule is evaluated per arm against that arm's own pilot controls when uncertainty is carried at the class level, and against the whole natural-control panel when it is carried at the sequence-cluster level (`src/transfer/generation_biology_analysis.py:392`). Nineteen arms report a structure contrast; two of them — ProLLaMA and ZymCTRL — have their own natural controls in the pilot. The other seventeen, which is every expansion arm and the ProGen3-3B native arm, carry `calibration_scope = shared_R232_natural_control_panel_not_native_class_calibration` and inherit attainment from those two. "Calibration attained, 116/116" is therefore a true statement about a two-arm panel and not about the seventeen arms whose positive contrasts it licenses. The inherited arms differ from the calibrated ones in generation procedure, class conditioning and length distribution, and the preregistration already forbids borrowing calibration across an unrepresented sequence regime without a limitation. This is that limitation, stated as a count rather than as a caveat.

The calibration rows were produced by a different runner revision from the rows they calibrate — `4d13435d4686…` against `ef04f7968e05…`. Their recorded contracts are identical on all 26 measurement fields and differ only in the code digests, so this does not put the calibration and the main panel on different measurements; it is a provenance fact, and it is the same fact that produced the receipt defect below.

## Receipts

Three of the four receipt conditions hold everywhere, one was broken by code, and one artifact class had no receipt at all.

**Digest binding holds.** All 46 shard cells' recorded `index_sha256` binds the index file beside it, and each shard's recorded row and status counts match its index. Every claimed prediction-file digest binds the bytes on disk: 13,524 of 13,524 files checked, 0 missing, 0 mismatched. The pooled predictions digests match the `input_sha256` the analyses recorded, and all seven cohort and ledger inputs match theirs.

**The signature did not bind its own contract on the calibration shards.** Four of 46 worker receipts record the evaluation signature `d795500931460f19…` while the contract printed in the same file digests to `fdd8144223a0…`. These four are the calibration shards, and they stamp all 116 calibration rows. The cause is a runner flag, `--pin-evaluation-signature`, added during the ESMFold-v1 to ESMFold2 migration so that a tree could be resumed after the runner file changed: it wrote an operator-supplied string where the digest belonged. This is a defect, not a migration consequence, because it makes a receipt assert a provenance nothing verifies.

It is a defect with no measurement consequence, and the reason is worth stating precisely rather than assumed. The signature was defined over the whole contract, including the SHA-256 of the runner and module files. All three recorded contracts — calibration, main/native, expansion — agree on every one of their 26 other fields: predictor identity, all six checkpoint shard digests, `num_loops=3`, 14 sampling steps, 32 diffusion samples, seed 20260905, bfloat16 with TF32 disabled, single sequence and no MSA, highest-pTM selection, the 0–1 to 0–100 conversion, the length bounds and the schema version. Dropping the code digests, all 46 receipts reduce to one measurement-contract digest, `06f8ef08ab940157…`. The two distinct signatures in the evidence therefore record two code revisions of one measurement, and the pinned value named a contract that was measurement-identical to the one it was written onto.

**The pooled expansion artifacts had no receipt of their own.** Seventeen of 20 pooled cohorts — every expansion arm — hold only a `predictions.jsonl`, with no index summary, no index digest and no prediction objects in that directory; the shard indexes were concatenated outside the runner's own index path. The 4,098 expansion rows' claimed file digests therefore bound nothing that was present beside them. This is a provenance gap rather than a defect: the digests bind correctly in the 34 shard trees, which this reconciliation verified, and the pooled files are row-identical to those trees. The pooled receipt was not regenerated, because the runner's index path needs the prediction objects beside the index and writing them into the pooled directories would replace the artifact each analysis actually read; the retrospective binding to the verified shard trees is recorded in its place.

## Reproducibility of the instrument

Nothing in the recorded evidence said whether folding the same sequence twice returns the same number, and the reported contrasts cannot be read without that. Eight of the recorded calibration rows, spanning 69 to 644 residues and both roles, were re-folded with the repaired runner from frozen snapshot `20260923203536_573eb2354393` on two idle H200 cards, against cohort digest `2eb701ea27c0abc7…`. Three cells ran, all `exited-ok` with 0 failures: an undivided 32-sample cell at seed 20260905, and two 16-sample cells at stream seeds 20260905 and 20260906, which are chunk 0 and chunk 1 of a 16+16 split, so the higher-pTM of that pair is the chunked measurement of the same 32 samples.

The re-fold cell's evaluation signature is `06f8ef08ab940157…`, the same measurement-contract digest all 46 historical receipts reduce to, so the new rows and the recorded rows are bound to one measurement. `--verify` passes on all three trees and binds both receipts in each.

| Repeat at the same settings and seed, against the recorded row | 8 | 0.081 | 0.929 | 0 of 8 |
| Chunked 16+16 against the undivided 32-sample call | 8 | 0.164 | 0.529 | — |

The instrument is therefore reproducible to about a pLDDT point per row and not bitwise: no prediction archive digest repeated, pTM agreed to within 0.0020, and the selected highest-pTM sample index differed in 5 of 8 rows, which is how near-equal pTM samples resolve differently between runs. The runner does not request deterministic kernels, and at bfloat16 the reductions are not order-stable, so this is expected rather than anomalous — but it was undeclared, and a single row's CA-pLDDT should not be quoted to more than about one point. The two largest repeat differences are on the two rows with the lowest pTM — 0.929 points on a 510-residue composition shuffle at pTM 0.247 and mean CA-pLDDT 33.914, and 0.394 points on the 69-residue natural control at pTM 0.611 — which is where the ranking among samples is flattest and the selected sample most easily changes.

Both quantities bound what the split and the repeat noise can contribute to a reported contrast. The smallest reported arm contrast is ProLLaMA at +6.115 points with a 95% interval half-width of 2.42 points over 125 clusters, and the largest is the ZymCTRL calibration at +65.195 points; a per-row repeat spread of at most 0.929 points and a per-row split cost of at most 0.529 points are far below both. The 56 chunked rows are 0.8% of the admitted rows and are concentrated in the longest sequences, so no arm's contrast rests on them.

## Repairs

Three root causes, one change each.

**The evaluation signature is now the digest of the measurement contract, and the override is gone.** `src/transfer/structure_evidence.py` gains `measurement_contract`, `contract_signature`, `manifest_signature` and `tree_signature`: the signature digests the contract without its code digests, code digests stay in the contract as provenance, and a worker receipt is read only through a check that its recorded signature is the digest of the contract printed beside it. `scripts/transfer/run_structure_evidence.py` drops `--pin-evaluation-signature` and computes the signature with `se.contract_signature`, and its `--index-only` path now derives the signature through `se.tree_signature` instead of trusting the recorded string. Editing a comment no longer invalidates rows whose measured quantity is unchanged, which is what made the override look necessary, so the exception is removed rather than replaced.

**The replica axis no longer splits across cards.** `_fold_replica_axis` folded chunks concurrently on several devices through a thread pool while seeding the process-global generator per chunk, so the preregistered rule that chunk `i` draws from stream seed 20260905 + `i` was not what would have run. The concurrent path is deleted; chunks stay on the assigned card and run one at a time, which is the only arrangement in which the declared per-chunk seed is the realized one. This was a latent defect: all 46 receipts record `cuda_visible_devices` unset, so the multi-device branch was never taken and 0 of 6,762 rows were produced through it. The same receipts correct the 2026-09-20 log line describing the six recovered calibration controls as split "across two cards": they were sequential chunks on one card.

**The realized chunking is reported, and a tree can be replayed against its own receipts.** `index_summary` now carries `diffusion_sample_splits_counts`, so a cohort that mixes sampling streams says so in its own summary. `verify_evidence` replays a tree without writing anything and raises on the first disagreement: a signature no receipt binds, a tree mixing signatures, a stored prediction whose bytes have moved away from the digest its row claims, a prediction object outside the cohort, a row set that differs from the recorded one, a row with no terminal result, or an index receipt whose digest does not match the replayed index. The runner exposes it as `--verify`, which needs no GPU and no checkpoint.

The retired ESMFold-v1 atom37 summary was removed in the same pass. It had no caller after the migration and described the instrument the programme had already retired.

## What the tests now enforce

`tests/test_structure_evidence.py` holds the conditions and both sides of each.

A worker receipt must carry the digest of the contract printed beside it: the operator-pinned value from the defect above is refused by name, the full-contract digest is refused because code digests are provenance and not the measurement, and a receipt without a contract is refused. The measurement signature must ignore code digests and nothing else — changing the code digests leaves it equal, changing the seed does not. A tree must resolve to exactly one receipt-bound signature: no receipt is refused, and two receipts disagreeing is refused with the count.

A replay must reproduce the recorded row set exactly and refuse every unbound artifact. The positive path replays a two-row tree and matches the recorded index digest. The negative paths are a row set that differs from the recorded one even when the extra row shares an already folded sequence, a prediction object directory outside the cohort, a stored prediction whose bytes were tampered with while its row was left untouched, a row left without a terminal result, and a tree with no index receipt to verify against.

A positive structure contrast is unreachable without attained calibration. With a strictly positive 97.5% interval on the contrast, an attained natural-control receipt yields the positive label and an absent, empty or unattained receipt yields `uncalibrated_predictor` in every case. The refusal is a label on the arm rather than an exception, because the pilot phase must be able to report an uncalibrated arm; that is a property of the design and it is recorded here rather than worked around.

## Irreducible limitations

Predictor confidence is not observed folding, expression or function; no exact-sequence experimental measurement exists for any row here, and none is available in this campaign. A composition shuffle calibrates a sequence-order alternative and not a profile or retrieval alternative, and it is not an experimentally certified nonfolder.

Seventeen of 19 arms inherit calibration from a two-arm natural-control panel. Attaining it per arm needs natural controls drawn in each arm's own class and length regime, which is a new pilot rather than a reanalysis.

The 28 `failed` rows are CUDA out-of-memory refusals on 716–954-residue sequences and remain in every denominator as unknown rather than as low confidence. On a 143,771 MiB card an undivided 32-sample call on those lengths reaches 131–135 GiB, so the long-sequence regime is reachable only through the chunked stream; that is a hardware bound, not a choice.

Repeat reproducibility is measured on 8 rows of one cohort, not on the 6,762 admitted rows; it bounds the per-row spread at these lengths and confidences and is not an interval on any reported estimate. The chunking cost is measured by reconstructing a 16+16 split from two 16-sample cells, which reproduces the declared stream seeds but not the memory conditions under which the original splits were forced.

The historical receipts predate the repaired signature rule, so `verify_evidence` refuses them by construction: they record full-contract digests, four of them do not bind even that, and the rows carry the same legacy values. They are verified instead by the recorded-digest, row-set and analysis-replay checks in this document, which is weaker than a single self-binding receipt and is the reason the retrospective measurement-contract digest is recorded rather than asserted. Rewriting a historical receipt so that it binds would manufacture provenance and was not done.

The analysis that consumes these rows does not itself re-check the evaluation signature (`src/transfer/generation_biology_analysis.py:130`), so a pool mixing signatures would not be refused there. After the repair the instrument cannot emit such a pool, and the condition is enforced at the instrument boundary rather than duplicated downstream.
